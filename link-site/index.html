Addresses for Casama servers people run at home.

Every address here, like k7qm2xfd9abc.casama.link, leads to a Casama server running on someone's own computer. Casama Link gives it that address so its owner's phone can reach it from anywhere, with no domain and no open ports.

Checked on this page only. It doesn't contact the server or tell anyone.

How it works

Your phone talks to your computer through Cloudflare. Casama Cloud hands out the address and checks who's signing in, but your chats and files never pass through it.

  1. Your phone Opens your address in the Casama app, on Wi-Fi or mobile data.
  2. https
  3. Cloudflare Carries the traffic down a tunnel your computer opened. No ports open at home.
  4. tunnel
  5. Your computer Runs your Casama server, your bots and their computers. Everything stays here.

Casama Cloud, off to the side: gives out the address, links the server to your account, signs in your phone by QR code, and passes on notifications.

For the owner

Turn it on from the desktop app (Settings → Casama Link → Use on Your Phone) or with casama selfhost remote on, then scan the QR code with your phone.

About Casama Link

Who can get in

Turning on an address closes sign-ups on that server, so whoever finds it can't make an account. The owner can let invited people sign up.

What Casama can and can't see

Casama Cloud keeps

  • Which workspace the server is linked to, its name, its address, the version it runs and when it last checked in.
  • The text of notifications as it passes them to Apple and Google, without storing it. The owner can turn on Private Notifications to keep even that on the server.

It never gets

  • Your chats, files, bots, memory or passwords. They live on your computer and travel through the tunnel, not through Casama's servers.
  • Live video of a bot's screen. The relay passes it encrypted between the bot's computer and your phone.

Casama runs the names and tunnels in its own Cloudflare account, so it can take an address down. The address is random, but it isn't a secret: owners should treat their server as public, with a good password and two-step sign-in. If you want no one else in the path, give your server your own domain or use a VPN. Read the details.

Report an address

Seen phishing, malware, spam or anything illegal on a casama.link address? Tell us. Casama doesn't run these servers, but it can turn an address off.

  • We read every report.
  • If the address breaks our terms, we turn it off. It stays off until we lift it.
  • Include the full address. Screenshots help.
