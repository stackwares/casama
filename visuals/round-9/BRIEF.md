# Brief for round 9: starter set covers for the app's "Picks for you" screen

Make exactly ONE image with your built-in image_gen tool (imagegen skill, built-in tool mode). Copy the exact
`savedPath` your image_gen call returned to the target path in your task, never "the newest file" in a
folder: several tasks run at once. Don't resize it, don't edit, commit or deploy anything else, and don't
touch other files in visuals/round-9/raw/.

What it's for: the first screen a new person sees after sign-up offers eight starter sets (bundles of
skills, routines and apps). Each set gets one cover. The cover shows small (a 64 px rounded square beside
the set's name) and large (a 320 px panel on tablets and desktops), so it needs ONE clear, simple subject
that still reads as a thumbnail. All eight must look like one family.

Format: square 1:1, 1024x1024. One object (or a small group) centered, filling about 55-60% of the frame,
resting on a soft, smooth background in the set's own color (a gentle two-tone gradient, a little paper
grain). Keep the outer 12% of every edge plain background: the image gets cropped into rounded squares.
Soft light from the upper left and a soft shadow under the object.

Style: the same family as Casama's mascot and wallpapers: soft, rounded, calm, slightly dreamy
illustration with a gentle 3D, almost clay-like softness, smooth shading, warm highlights. Friendly and
premium, like a polished app onboarding illustration. Not photo-realistic, no neon, no glitter overload,
no AI clichés (no brains, circuits, robots, holograms).

HARD RULES: no text, letters, numbers, signs with writing, or logos anywhere (paper and screens stay
blank, envelopes have no stamps with text); no people, faces, hands, mascots or characters; no app UI,
phones, laptops or screens.

Reply with two lines: the image_gen savedPath, and the target path you copied it to.
