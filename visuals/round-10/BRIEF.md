# Brief for round 10: painted pictures across the app

Make exactly ONE image with your built-in image_gen tool (imagegen skill, built-in tool mode). Copy the exact
`savedPath` your image_gen call returned to the target path in your task, never "the newest file" in a
folder: several tasks run at once. Don't resize it, don't edit, commit or deploy anything else, and don't
touch other files in visuals/round-10/raw/.

What it's for: Casama's app already has eight painted "starter set" covers (visuals/round-9). These new
pictures carry that look to more screens: empty screens, pickers, the waitlist and invite sheets, the
paywall. Each picture shows small (a 48-64 px rounded square) and large (a 320 px panel or a 4:3 tile), so
it needs ONE clear, simple subject that still reads as a thumbnail. All pictures must look like one
family with the round-9 covers.

Format: square 1:1, 1024x1024. One object (or a small group) centered, filling about 55-60% of the frame,
resting on a soft, smooth background in the color your task names (a gentle two-tone gradient, a little
paper grain). Keep the outer 12% of every edge plain background: the image gets cropped into rounded
squares and 4:3 tiles, so nothing important may sit near the top or bottom edge either.
Soft light from the upper left and a soft shadow under the object.

Style: soft, rounded, calm, slightly dreamy illustration with a gentle 3D, almost clay-like softness,
smooth shading, warm highlights. Friendly and premium, like a polished app onboarding illustration. Not
photo-realistic, no neon, no glitter overload, no AI clichés (no brains, circuits, robots, holograms).

HARD RULES: no text, letters, numbers, signs with writing, or logos anywhere (paper and screens stay
blank, envelopes have no stamps with text); no people, faces, hands, mascots or characters; no app UI and
no phones or laptops (a task may allow one blank, rounded desktop computer: then its screen is a plain
soft glow with nothing on it).

Reply with two lines: the image_gen savedPath, and the target path you copied it to.
