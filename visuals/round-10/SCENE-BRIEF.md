# Brief for round 10: the splash and welcome background scene

Make exactly ONE image with your built-in image_gen tool (imagegen skill, built-in tool mode). Copy the
exact `savedPath` your image_gen call returned to the target path in your task, never "the newest file"
in a folder: several tasks run at once. Don't resize, crop or edit it, and touch only your target file.

What it's for: the full-screen background behind Casama's app splash and its first "getting ready"
screen. Casama's animated mascot (a small round character) sits in the middle of the screen, with a line
of text and a progress spinner under it. So the middle of the picture must be calm, open and even: soft
sky or soft light, no objects, no strong focal point. Interest lives near the edges and the bottom third.

Scene: "coming home". A wide, peaceful painted landscape: soft rolling hills along the bottom with a
few rounded trees, a tiny cosy cottage with a warm lit window far off on one side, a winding path, and a
big open sky with a few soft clouds filling most of the frame. Gentle, dreamy, a little magical.

Style: the same family as Casama's painted wallpapers and set covers: soft, rounded, calm illustration
with a gentle painterly, slightly clay-like softness, smooth gradients, a little paper grain, warm
highlights. Low contrast overall so a mascot and text read on top. Not photo-realistic, no neon, no
glitter overload, no AI clichés.

Day version (light mode): bright, airy, soft pastel morning light (lavender, peach, pale sky blue,
soft greens). No deep shadows or dark patches.
Night version (dark mode): the SAME scene (give the day painting named in your task as a reference
image: same composition, same hills, trees, cottage and path in the same places, same style); only the
time of day and light change. Deep, quiet and fairly dark: muted deep blues, plums, inky greens, a soft
moon glow, a few small stars, the cottage window glowing warm. No bright patches or hard white highlights.

HARD RULES: no text, letters, numbers, signs or logos; no people, faces, animals, mascots or characters;
no app UI, phones or screens; nothing busy in the middle of the frame.

Reply with two lines: the image_gen savedPath, and the target path you copied it to.
