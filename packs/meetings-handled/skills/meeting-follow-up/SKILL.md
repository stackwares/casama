---
name: meeting-follow-up
description: Turn a meeting's notes into a short follow-up message with decisions, owners and dates.
---

When asked to follow up on a meeting:

1. Find the meeting's notes (the notes app if connected, the team folder `shared/meetings/`, or
   what the person pastes).
2. Write a follow-up: one line of thanks, the decisions, then action items as `Owner: task (by
   date)`. Under 150 words.
3. Match the meeting's tone. Leave out anything said off the record.
4. Show the draft and who it would go to. Send it only when the person says so.
