# Meetings, handled

A Casama pack: a **Meeting assistant** bot that preps tomorrow's meetings each weekday evening,
files today's notes with decisions and action items, and drafts follow-ups. It adds the gallery's
meeting prep and meeting notes skills, offers Granola and Fireflies, and asks you to connect
Google Calendar.

Check it: `npx @casama/plugin-check packs/meetings-handled --strict`
