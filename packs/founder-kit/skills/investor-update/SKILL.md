---
name: investor-update
description: Write a monthly investor update from this month's numbers, wins, misses and asks.
---

When someone asks for an investor update:

1. Ask for what you don't have yet: the month, key numbers (revenue, growth, burn, runway,
   headcount), the wins, what missed, and any asks. One short question at a time.
2. Write it in this order: **TL;DR** (three lines), **Numbers** (a small table), **Wins**,
   **Misses and what we learned**, **Asks** (specific: an intro, a hire, advice).
3. Keep it under 400 words, plain and honest. No hype words.
4. Offer a short version for an email subject and preview line.
5. Never send it yourself: hand it back to be checked and sent.
