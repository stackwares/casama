# Casama trademark policy

DRAFT for legal review.

Casama's code is open source, but its name isn't. "Casama", the Casama logo and the bot mascots
("the Marks") are trademarks of Stackwares, used in commerce since November 2026 and marked
"Casama™" until registered. The open-source licenses cover the code, not the Marks: the AGPL-3.0
(section 7(e)) and the Apache License (section 6) grant no rights to them. This policy says how you
may use them.

The aim is simple: people should be able to tell whether something comes from us.

## Fine without asking

- **Saying what's true.** "Runs on Casama", "a plugin for Casama", "compatible with Casama",
  "a fork of Casama", or writing about Casama in articles, reviews, tutorials and talks.
- **Unchanged copies.** Redistributing an unmodified Casama release, or self-hosting an unmodified
  Casama server for yourself, your family or your organisation, under the Casama name.
- **Plugins, skills, templates and gallery items** that say they're for Casama, as long as their
  own name doesn't start with or look like "Casama" (use "Notes for Casama", not "Casama Notes").
- **Community events and groups** about Casama that are free to attend and say they're
  unofficial.

## Needs our written permission

- **A modified version under the Casama name.** If you change the code and distribute it or offer
  it to others, give it your own name and logo. You may say it's "based on Casama".
- **A hosted service for other people** with "Casama" in its name, domain, app name or store
  listing.
- **Names that look like ours,** such as Kasama-style spellings, "Casama Pro", or a logo or mascot
  that could be mistaken for ours.
- **Merchandise** and anything sold with the Marks on it.
- **Suggesting we endorse or partner with you** when we don't.

## Forks

Forks are welcome; that's what open source is for. Before you publish a fork, change the app name,
bundle and package ids, logo, mascots, store listings, domains and the default links to casama.ai
services. Keep the copyright and license notices and the [NOTICE](NOTICE) file, and offer your
users the source of your fork under AGPL-3.0, as the licenses require.

## Asking

Write to hello@casama.ai with what you'd like to do. We say yes to most reasonable requests.

We may update this policy. Use that was fine when you started stays fine unless it misleads people.
