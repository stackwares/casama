features:
  - title: Bots that reach out first
    details: Each bot has instructions, memory, skills and a budget. It sends a morning brief, spots things it could do for you, and keeps working on schedules and triggers while you're away.
    link: /guide/bots
    linkText: Bots and models
  - title: A real computer each
    details: A sandboxed Linux computer with a shell, files and a browser. Watch it live, take control at any time, or lend a bot your own machine.
    link: /guide/computers
    linkText: Computers
  - title: Everything it makes, kept
    details: Sites, games, reports, documents, pictures, videos and sounds land in the Library, grouped by project and shareable with a link.
    link: /guide/library
    linkText: The Library
  - title: Sites at their own address
    details: Publish a site a bot made in one tap, at a free address or on your own domain. On Casama Cloud, buy a domain in the app with the DNS set up for you.
    link: /guide/sites-and-domains
    linkText: Sites and domains
  - title: You stay in charge
    details: Autonomy levels, approvals for risky actions, action rules, checkpoints before destructive commands and a full timeline of every run.
    link: /guide/autonomy
    linkText: Autonomy and approvals
  - title: Where you already work
    details: Apps for iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows, Linux and the web, plus Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, email and more, Linear and Jira issues, voice calls, and your AI coding tools.
    link: /guide/chat-apps
    linkText: Chat apps
  - title: Never more than a glance away
    details: A working bot shows in your Mac's notch and menu bar and on your phone's Lock Screen, with Stop, Allow and Deny right there. Casama for Chrome brings your bots to the page you're on.
    link: /guide/notch
    linkText: Notch and menu bar
  - title: Calls that stay with you
    details: Call a bot live, minimise the call and keep using the app, or lock the phone and keep talking. Watch its computer during the call, or let a bot ring your phone.
    link: /guide/voice
    linkText: Voice and calls
  - title: Works with your tools
    details: One-click app connections, MCP servers, plugins and password managers, with secrets kept out of the model's sight. Plus an API, CLI and SDK to build on.
    link: /guide/connections
    linkText: Apps and connections
  - title: Any model
    details: Bring your own key for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, OpenRouter or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, run local models for free, or sign in with a ChatGPT plan on your own server.
    link: /guide/bots
    linkText: Choose a model
  - title: Yours to run
    details: Open source (AGPL-3.0), one install script, and no paywall when you self-host. Casama Link puts a server on your own computer on your phone, with no domain and no open ports.
    link: /self-hosting/
    linkText: Self-hosting
  - title: Or let us run it
    details: Casama Cloud is the hosted service, with a free plan and paid plans from $15 a month. Casama picks the models, so there are no keys to add, and models, computers and voice are paid from credits.
    link: /guide/usage
    linkText: Plans and usage
