# Appearance and themes

Make Casama look the way you like: pick a whole theme in one tap, then change its colors,
background and fonts. Everything lives in one place, **Settings → Appearance**, under **App**.
Your look is saved on each device, so your phone and laptop can look different.

## Pick a theme

1. Open **Settings**, and under **App**, tap **Appearance**.
2. Choose **Light**, **System** or **Dark**.
3. Under **Themes**, tap a theme card. Each card is drawn in the theme's own colors, fonts and
   background, so what you see is what you get.

The cards show your own themes first, then Casama's, then **From the Gallery** (tap **Show All**
for the rest). The Casama theme is the look a new install starts with.

Trying themes never loses work. If tapping a theme replaces changes you hadn't saved, a message
offers **Undo**.

At the top, a small chat shows the look in use, with its name, or **Custom look** once you've
changed it. Tap **Save** there to keep a custom look as one of your themes.

## Customize a look

Tap **Customize** under the preview. The page has three tabs, under a live preview that stays in
view:

| Tab | What you can change |
| --- | --- |
| **Colors** | The color scheme. Search, or filter by family; the **Gallery** chip adds schemes from the gallery. |
| **Background** | Wallpapers from the gallery, a doodle **Pattern** and its **Pattern Strength**, **Colors**, or **Your Photo** (with **Blur** and **Dim** so text stays readable). Turn off **Show on every screen** to keep it behind chats only. **Reset to Default** goes back. |
| **Fonts** | **Headings** and **Text**. Google Sans and Noto Serif work offline; other Google Fonts download the first time you pick them. **Use the Defaults** goes back. |

The sun and moon button in the header previews the other brightness without changing your
setting. Once the look is your own, **Save** appears in the header.

## Share and import themes

- **Share code:** in one of your themes' menu, copy a code that starts with `casama-theme:`.
  Anyone can paste it into **Appearance → Import a Theme**.
- **Share to Gallery:** propose your look for everyone, through the
  [open gallery](https://gallery.casama.ai).
- **Delete** removes one of your own themes.
- A gallery theme's menu has **About this theme**, with who made it.

A gallery theme you use is kept with your themes, so it works offline.

## Fewer effects

Turn on **Fewer Effects** to drop celebrations, bouncing tabs and animated bots. Casama also
follows your system's reduced motion setting.

## Ask a bot for a new look

Bots can suggest a look, such as "make it calmer" or "something dark and green". The suggestion
shows as a card in the chat; **Apply** changes the look on that device only, with **Undo**. See
[Bots that change their own setup](/guide/bots#bots-that-change-their-own-setup).

## Make your own

**Make a look** on the gallery ([gallery.casama.ai/make.html](https://gallery.casama.ai/make.html))
builds a theme, color scheme or wallpaper in your browser, with live contrast checks and light and
dark previews. It gives you the files to propose on GitHub.

## Limits

- Your look stays on the device where you set it. It doesn't follow you to your other devices; use
  a theme code to copy it.
- Some newer gallery looks need a newer version of the app. Older versions hide the ones they
  can't draw.
