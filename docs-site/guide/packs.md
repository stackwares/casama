# Packs

A pack sets up a whole job in one go: one or more bots with their instructions, the skills they
use, routines that run on a schedule, the apps they work best with and sometimes a look for the
app. Adding a pack makes ordinary bots, skills and routines, so you can change or remove any of
them afterwards like anything you made yourself.

## Find a pack

- **Gallery → Packs:** packs Casama has reviewed, such as **Founder kit**, **Dev kit** and
  **Meetings handled**. Each card says what's inside ("1 bot · 1 skill · 2 routines").
- **From a link:** **Gallery → Add a pack from a link**, then paste a GitHub link someone gave you.
  A pack link on the web (`casama.ai/open/pack?url=…`) opens the same page in the app.
- **From a file:** **Gallery → Add from a file** and pick a pack's `.zip`.
- **From a share link:** someone shared their bots with you as a pack. The link opens it in the app
  for anyone signed in to Casama.

## Before you add it

Nothing is added until you say so. The pack's page shows:

- who made it and whether Casama reviewed it. A pack Casama hasn't reviewed says so in red, and
  its routines start switched off until you turn them on;
- every bot, skill, routine, app and look it brings. Open a bot or routine to read its full
  instructions;
- **What it can reach**: the sites it talks to, whether it runs code on a bot's computer, the keys
  it asks for, and roughly how many scheduled runs a week it makes. Casama works this out from the
  pack's files, not from what the author says.

Press **Add pack** and you can untick anything you don't want, answer the pack's questions (your
team's name, what time a routine should run), and enter any keys it needs. The last step lists
what was added, with **Connect** for apps it works best with and **Use** for a look.

Bots from a pack always start by asking before risky actions. Your workspace's gallery rules
(**Workspace settings → Gallery**) apply to packs too: if an admin has to approve gallery items,
your request waits for them.

## Updates and removing

**Gallery → Installed → Installed packs** lists your packs and what each added.

- **Check for update** shows what changed, item by item. Anything you edited since adding the pack
  stays yours unless you choose the pack's version. If the update reaches new sites or asks for
  new keys, it says so and waits for you to confirm.
- **Remove** takes away the pack's tools and the items you never changed. Your bots stay unless
  you tick **Also delete the bots this pack created**, and anything you edited stays too.

## Share your bots as a pack

**Workspace settings → Share as pack** turns up to five of your bots into a pack, with their
instructions, skills and scheduled routines, the apps they use (by name only) and, if you like,
this device's look. Keys, chats, memories, About you and your model choice never go in. Before
sharing, Casama checks every file for anything that looks like a key or password and shows you
the lines.

You get a link that works for 30 days for anyone signed in to Casama, and you can stop sharing it
at any time. **Save as file** gives you the pack as a `.zip` instead.
