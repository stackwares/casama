# Domain terms and prices

These terms apply when you buy a domain through Casama (version 2026-11-16). They sit alongside
Casama's [terms of service](https://casama.ai/terms) and [privacy policy](https://casama.ai/privacy).

## Who you're dealing with

- Casama resells domain registrations. The **sponsoring registrar** is **Name.com, Inc.**, an
  ICANN-accredited registrar. Your domain is registered with you (or your business) as the
  registrant, using the details you enter.
- By buying, you also agree to the registrar's registration agreement and the rules of the
  registry for the domain's ending, which Casama passes on as required.
- ICANN publishes what registrants are entitled to and what they're responsible for:
  [Registrant Benefits and Responsibilities](https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/benefits-2013-09-16-en)
  and [Registrant Educational Information](https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/educational-2012-02-25-en).

## What you're buying

- A registration for **one year**, renewing each year while **Renew automatically** is on and the
  renewal is paid. You can turn renewal off at any time on the domain's screen.
- DNS for the domain, run by the registrar and managed in Casama, and free registration privacy:
  your details are redacted from public lookups.
- The domain is yours. You can move it to another registrar from the domain's screen once the
  60-day transfer lock after registration ends; nothing about leaving depends on your plan.

## Your duties

- Give accurate registrant details and keep them current in **Contact Details**. Confirm the
  registrant email when the registrar asks, within 15 days, or the domain stops working.
- Don't use the domain for phishing, malware, spam or anything illegal. Casama and the registrar
  suspend domains used for abuse; see Casama's terms of service.

## Payment, renewals and refunds

- Prices are in US dollars, before tax; tax is added at checkout where it applies.
- **Sales are final once the domain is registered.** If a paid domain can't be registered, the
  full amount is refunded automatically.
- With **Renew automatically** on, the renewal is charged about 14 days before the expiry date to
  the payment method kept from your purchase, and tried again on days 10, 7 and 3 if it fails.
  Notices go out a month, a week and a day before.
- A domain that isn't renewed expires. It can still be renewed at the normal renewal price for 25
  days after its expiry date. After that it may be restorable through Casama's support, at the
  registry's restore fee, for as long as the registry allows; this isn't guaranteed.

## Prices

Every price is worked out the same way from what the registrar charges Casama that day:
**cost × 1.25 + $1.50, rounded up to the next .99**. Search shows the first year's price and the
renewal price for each name before you buy, and each domain's screen shows its next renewal price.

| | Price |
|---|---|
| First year | As shown in **Find a Domain** for that name |
| Renewal | As shown on the domain's screen and in the 30-day notice; the same formula on that day's cost |
| Late renewal (up to 25 days after expiry) | The same as renewal: no extra fee |
| Restore (after 25 days, through support) | The registry's restore fee plus one year's renewal, quoted before you pay |

Premium names (priced higher by their registry) and endings with residency rules aren't sold.
