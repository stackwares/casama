# Privacy and data

- **Export**: Settings → Privacy → Export my data (or `kasama account export`): a ZIP of JSON files
  with everything you can see. Secrets and keys are never included.
- **Delete**: Settings → Privacy → Delete my account. You are signed out at once; your data,
  workspaces you solely own and their computer snapshots are erased after the grace period.
- **Retention**: workspace admins choose how long run history is kept (default 180 days).
- **Telemetry**: off. Self-hosters can opt in to a weekly anonymous ping (instance id, version, two
  counts).
