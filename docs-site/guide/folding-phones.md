# Casama on a folding phone

On an iPhone Duo, Casama follows the device: one layout for the outer screen, two panes on the
inner screen, and its own layouts for when the phone is partly folded. Whatever you were doing is
still there when you open or close it. Android phones that fold get the same two panes.

## Closed: the outer screen

iOS keeps a strip down the right edge for the camera, the clock and the battery. Casama puts its
buttons there too, as Apple's apps do, so the page gets the full height:

- **At the top of the strip:** Back, then the page's buttons (in a chat: call, new topic and more).
- **At the bottom:** Chats, Library and Tasks, and the button for connecting apps.

The page's title stays at the top of the page.

## Open: two panes

Held wide, the inner screen shows the list on the left and what you opened on the right. They meet
on the fold.

- **Chats:** the chat list beside the chat. Opening the device on the chat list shows your last
  chat beside it. If you close the device without touching that chat, you are back on the list.
- **Library and Settings:** the list on the left, the item or the page on the right.
- **Tasks:** the board, with one column on each side of the fold. Swipe for the next columns.
- **More room for the chat:** the first button in the strip hides the list. Tap it again to bring
  the list back.
- **The bot's computer beside its chat:** tap the monitor button in a chat. The computer takes the
  list's place; tap the button again for the list.

Held tall, the inner screen shows the phone layout.

## Partly folded

While the phone is folded part-way, nothing you can tap sits on the fold.

- **Like a book:** the two panes stay, with the band around the fold left empty. Pages that have
  no list of their own show on the right half with your chats on the left. Sheets and messages
  open on the right half.
- **Like a laptop, watching a bot's computer:** the computer's screen fills the top half. The
  bottom half is a trackpad: one finger moves the pointer, two fingers scroll, a tap clicks. The
  right-click button makes your next tap a right click. The keyboard and **Hand Back** are there
  too. This needs you to be in control of the computer first: tap **Take Control**.
- **Like a laptop, on a call:** the bot and the live captions are on the top half, the buttons on
  the bottom half.
- **Like a tent:** the outer screen on its side. A call shows the bot and the captions large, with
  the buttons beside them, and the screen stays on.

## Opening and closing

The chat you are in, where you had scrolled to, what you were typing and the keyboard stay as they
are. A sheet that is open stays open and moves to where it belongs on the other screen. A call or a
live view of a bot's computer carries on.

## Beside another app

In Split View Casama shows the phone layout. Drag a photo, a file or some text from the other app
onto a chat to attach it or add it to your message.

## If something looks wrong

**Settings → Folding screen** shows how Casama reads the device right now: the pose, the fold and
the strip. It only appears on a phone that folds. Tap the copy button and send us the numbers at
[hello@casama.ai](mailto:hello@casama.ai) with a screenshot.
