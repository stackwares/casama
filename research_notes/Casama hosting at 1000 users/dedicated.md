# Dedicated servers for many Docker containers

Checked 11 November 2026. Figures below are from the provider page or the official order catalog fetched that day. Auction rows are one snapshot of a feed that changes. Prices are monthly unless noted. “Excl. VAT” and “incl. VAT” are the page’s own labels.

## What are the price, cores, RAM, disk, bandwidth, and root or Docker terms around 64 GB and 128 GB?

### Takeaway

Hetzner, OVHcloud (Kimsufi, So you Start, Rise, Advance), and Contabo sell physical dedicated servers with root or full administrative control in the EU, so Docker on that machine is the customer’s operating system rather than a host ban. Netcup’s root servers in this RAM range are KVM virtual machines with root inside the guest, not a dedicated physical host. On 11 November 2026 the orderable new-server lists start near €26 excl. VAT for 64 GB (OVH Kimsufi, HDD) and near €34 excl. VAT for 128 GB (OVH Kimsufi, small NVMe). Hetzner’s new AX41 is €59 excl. VAT for 64 GB NVMe. Hetzner’s displayed AX102 is €259 excl. VAT for 128 GB, not €159.

### Inferences

- Hetzner AX, the Hetzner auction, OVH dedicated ranges, and Contabo dedicated are physical machines. The pages give root or full administrative control and do not state a ban on Docker. Contabo lists Docker as an install option. On Netcup’s root servers, Docker would run inside the KVM guest; the page says the guest is one virtual machine among others on a host.
- Robot’s “incl. 19% VAT” prices for AX41, the €99 AX42, and the €259 AX102 match the product pages’ excl. VAT prices times 1.19. The same factor is used above only where a Robot row was read in full.
- OVH memory add-ons with a €0 renew price are the included RAM. A positive renew price is an extra monthly amount on top of the plan. KS-5 at 64 GB is €17.99 + €8.00 = €25.99 excl. VAT per month. KS-5-B at 128 GB is €21.99 + €12.00 = €33.99 excl. VAT per month. Those totals are that addition, not a single price printed on a marketing card.
- The Ireland catalog’s 23% tax rate matches the Advance page’s incl. VAT figure. It is not the German 19% rate. A German OVH subsidiary was not fetched.

### Inferences

- The project figure of €159 per month for 16 cores and 128 GB matches the matrix’s lower “max/mo” number and does not match the product page or any AX102 row on the order page. The orderable 16-core, 128 GB, 2× 1.92 TB AX102 is €259 per month excl. VAT, setup €129 excl. VAT. The cheapest 128 GB ECC AX102 on the order page is the LTD row at €279 per month excl. 19% VAT, setup €39 excl. 19% VAT.
- A 128 GB auction server at €114 in the same day’s feed is cheaper than every new AX102 row, with the usual auction limits: one refurbished machine, rescue-system delivery, price not fixed.

### Gaps

- The matrix does not say which of the four AX102 types is €159, and the order page has no row at that price. The €159 cell may be stale. That was not confirmed against an older capture.
- Robot does not label AX102-1 RAM as ECC or non-ECC. The product page’s range line says “DDR5 ECC”. The catalog variation list includes both a 128 GB row and a 128 GB ECC row under one €259 price field, so which of those two is the €259 AX102-1 is not separated.
