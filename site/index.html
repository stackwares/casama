Its own computer to work on.

A private computer with a browser, files and apps, kept apart from your own devices. Watch it work or take over the mouse. It sleeps when idle and wakes in seconds.

How a task goes.

Say you ask Anna to book dinner for the team on Friday near the office, under $60 a head, and send the invite.

  1. You ask in plain words.

    Type it, say it, or forward an email. Put it on the board if it can wait.

    Checks 8 calendars: Friday 7 pm works. Remembers Maya is vegetarian.

  2. It works on its own computer.

    It browses, compares prices, fills in forms and writes files, without touching your own devices.

    Lupa is fully booked. Osteria Nove: 6 vegetarian dishes, $52 a head.

  3. It stops before anything risky.

    Sending, booking, paying or deleting waits for your OK, on your phone or here.

    Book Osteria Nove for 8 at 7 pm and send the invite? Checkpoint 12 saved.

  4. It reports back with the cost.

    Every step it took, and what it cost, down to a tenth of a cent.

Safe enough to hand it real work.

Every bot works on a locked-down computer of its own, reaches only the sites and accounts you allow, and saves a checkpoint before anything risky. If the server restarts mid-task, the work picks up where it left off.

What keeps you safe

  • Your workspace stays yours. Every table in the database has a rule, and a test proving that no other workspace can read your data.
  • Locked-down computers. Bots work on separate, restricted computers. They can't reach your own devices, or any file you didn't share.
  • Passwords the AI never sees. Logins from 1Password or Bitwarden are filled in on the way out, and only for the sites you allow.
  • Your data, portable. Optional encryption at rest, retention you set, and a full workspace export whenever you want it.

Questions, answered.

How is Casama different from a chatbot?

A chatbot answers in a text box. A Casama bot has its own computer, your tools, a memory and a schedule, so it does the work itself: books the table, fills in the form, drafts the email, and comes back when it is done or needs you.

Will a bot do something I didn't want?

You decide what each bot may do, action by action: allow, allow within limits, or ask first. Risky steps such as sending, booking, paying or deleting wait for your OK, and the computer is saved first so you can roll back.

Which AI models does it use?

On Casama Cloud, Casama picks and runs the models for you. When you self-host, bring keys for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, xAI or OpenRouter, sign in with your ChatGPT plan, or run local models with Ollama. Each bot can have a main model and a fallback.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. Your chats, memories and files are used to run Casama for you and for nothing else. We don't sell data or show ads, your keys never reach the model, and you can export everything or delete your account at any time.

What do credits pay for?

On Casama Cloud, credits are dollars. They pay for AI models, cloud computer time, live calls, images and other metered services. Every plan includes monthly credits, and paid plans can buy credit packs. A self-hosted server uses no credits: you pay your providers directly.

Can I run it on my own server?

Yes. Casama is open source under AGPL-3.0. One command installs the whole stack with Docker on a Linux server, a home server or a Mac, or start it from the Mac and Linux app. Self-hosting is free, with no limit on users or bots.

Why is Casama Cloud invite-only?

We're letting people in as we add capacity, so Casama stays fast for everyone who is in. Running Casama on your own server is open to everyone, with no invite and no waitlist.

How do I get an invite?

Join the waitlist at casama.ai/waitlist and we'll email you when it's your turn. Or ask someone who uses Casama: every member has a few invites to share.

I have an invite code. What now?

Go to casama.ai/waitlist and enter it with your email. Then sign in with that address. Google, Apple and the email link all work, as long as it's the same address.

Can I use my own server from my phone?

Yes. Turn on Casama Link and your server gets its own address, with no domain, port forwarding or VPN to set up. Scan a QR code with the Casama app to sign in, and notifications reach your phone like any other app's. Your traffic goes straight to your computer, never through Casama's servers. It's free on every plan; watching a bot's computer live while you're away from home comes with Plus and up.

How is Casama different from Grok Bot?

Grok Bot is xAI's hosted agent, on paid plans from $30 a month with no free plan. All of a person's Bots share one cloud computer, you can't choose or see the model, and you can't host it yourself. Casama gives each bot its own computer, has a free plan, and is open source: self-host it for free with any model, or use Casama Cloud. Grok Bot still has a larger template catalogue and can learn a workflow from one recording. To move a Bot, paste its setup into Casama and review what comes over. Casama vs Grok Bot

Can I build on Casama?

Yes. Everything the apps do goes through an open HTTP API, with a CLI and SDKs for TypeScript and Dart. Give bots new skills and tools with plugins, share whole bots and their routines as packs, or let other agents hand them work. Building with Claude Code, Codex or Cursor? Our agent kit gives your coding agent Casama's docs and API reference, no account needed.
