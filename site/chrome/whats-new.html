What's new in Casama for Chrome

The browser extension for your Casama bots. How it works

Version 0.1

  • Ask about any page. Open the side panel and ask a bot about the tab you're on. The page's address and your selection go with the message; switch on the page's text, a PDF's text, a video's captions or a screenshot when you want them. "What was sent" under each message shows exactly what went.
  • Replies stream in, with the bot's cards, Stop, Copy and Read aloud.
  • Save to the Library as Markdown, a PDF or a screenshot, into a project if you like. Remember a selection, add a page to a bot's knowledge, or make a task from it.
  • Right-click, shortcuts and the address bar. Ask about a selection, save a link or an image, take a screenshot of part of the page, or type ca then your question in the address bar.
  • The Inbox. Approve or deny a bot's step, answer its secret requests, accept suggestions and stop runs, from the panel or straight from a notification.
  • Settings you control. Choose where the extension may read pages, keep a never-list, and see every browser signed in to your account.
