## What we asked

On November 18, 2026, through an API with web search on and the country set to the United States:

1. What are the best open-source AI agents that get their own computer and can be self-hosted?
2. I want an AI assistant that does real tasks for me on its own computer, works while I am away,
   and asks before doing anything risky. What should I use?
3. What are the best alternatives to OpenClaw?
4. Rakazo vs MausBot vs OpenBot: which AI teammate platform should I pick?

That is twelve answers. We also asked Gemini; all of its calls failed that day, so it is left out.
One run each: assistants give different lists every time, so read this as a snapshot.

## Who they named

| Question | ChatGPT | Claude | Perplexity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open-source agents with their own computer | Hermes Agent, Agent Zero, Open WebUI | Agent Zero, Dify, OpenHands | Agent Zero |
| An assistant that works while I'm away and asks first | ChatGPT agent, OpenBot, Hermes Agent | OpenClaw, Manus, Muse, Dots | ChatGPT agent |
| Alternatives to OpenClaw | Hermes Agent, Manus | Hermes Agent, Manus | Hermes Agent, Agent Zero, n8n |
| Rakazo vs MausBot vs OpenBot | the three in the question | the three, plus Grok Bot and Lindy | the three in the question |

The table lists the names we checked for, so it is a floor, not a full count.

Casama appeared in none of the twelve. We expected that: the site was a week old.

## Three things we didn't expect

**The newest products barely register.** Rakazo, MausBot, OpenBot, Instinct and Hark Pro were
named once between them in the nine answers that didn't already mention them. Assistants reach for
the names with the most written about them: OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, Agent Zero, Manus.

**"Its own computer" isn't understood yet.** Asked for agents that get their own computer, two
assistants answered with developer frameworks and chat interfaces. The idea that an agent can have
a machine of its own, apart from yours, hasn't settled into how these models sort the field.

**Asking by name works.** Asked "What is Casama AI?", all three described it correctly, citing our
package page, our repository and our site. The assistants can find a new product. They don't yet
have a reason to bring it up.

## What they cited

Across the answers, the pages cited most were GitHub repositories, vendors' own sites, small
directories of open-source tools, community posts and roundup articles. For the three-way
comparison, the sources were the three vendors' own pages. Nobody independent had written that
comparison, so the assistants had nothing else to read.

## What we're doing about it

- Writing the comparisons that don't exist, with a source and a date on every fact about another
  product, and a section on when the other product is the better choice. They are under
  [alternatives](/alternatives/).
- Explaining the idea plainly: [why give an AI agent its own computer](/learn/why-give-an-ai-agent-its-own-computer).
- Repeating these questions every week and publishing how the answers change.

## Try it yourself

Ask your assistant the four questions above. If it names a product, ask it where it read that.
The sources tell you more than the answer.
