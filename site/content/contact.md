## Get an answer now

- **Casama Help** answers questions about Casama straight away, from our docs. Ask it on the
  [help page](/help) or with the **Questions** button at the bottom of any page.
- **Signed in?** Open **Settings → Help & support** in the app. There, Casama Help can also check
  your plan, purchases, usage and connected apps, and hand your problem to our support team with one
  tap.
- **Is something down?** [status.casama.ai](https://status.casama.ai) shows whether the API, web
  app, sign-in and docs are working right now.

## Email us

| For | Email |
|---|---|
| Help with the apps or your account | [support@casama.ai](mailto:support@casama.ai) |
| Plans, invoices and refunds for web and desktop purchases | [billing@casama.ai](mailto:billing@casama.ai) |
| Your data, privacy requests and account deletion | [privacy@casama.ai](mailto:privacy@casama.ai) |
| Security issues | [security@casama.ai](mailto:security@casama.ai) (see our [security policy](https://github.com/stackwares/casama/blob/main/SECURITY.md)) |
| Press, partnerships and anything else | [hello@casama.ai](mailto:hello@casama.ai) |

Write from the email address on your Casama account when it's about your account, so we can find it.
If we ask about a purchase, include the **workspace ID** from **Settings → Help & support**: it is
also your purchase ID in the App Store and Google Play. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, card
numbers or API keys. We will never ask for them.

## Purchases made in a store

Subscriptions bought in the App Store or Google Play are billed, cancelled and refunded by Apple or
Google, not by us:

- **App Store:** cancel in **Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions** on your iPhone, and ask for a
  refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
- **Google Play:** cancel and ask for a refund in **Play Store → Payments & subscriptions**.

For purchases on the web or in the desktop app, email
[billing@casama.ai](mailto:billing@casama.ai).
