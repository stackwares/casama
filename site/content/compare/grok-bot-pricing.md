## Grok Bot plans and prices

| Plan | Price | What the usage is |
|---|---|---|
| **Premium** | $30 a month | "Weekly Grok Bot usage included". The amount isn't stated. |
| **Super** | $100 a month | Everything in Premium, plus 3.5 times Premium's usage and priority model access at peak times. |
| **Ultra** | $200 a month | Everything in Super, plus 12 times Premium's usage and earliest access to all products and models. |

The [Grok Bot page](https://x.ai/bot) shows monthly prices only. It doesn't show a yearly price.
Premium, Super and Ultra are X subscriptions, and Grok Bot is included on each of them. Plus and
Free X accounts don't include it, according to the
[Grok Bot FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq).

## How the weekly pool works

- **One shared pool.** Grok Bot draws on one weekly pool of usage that it shares with Cursor and the
  Grok apps, says the [FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq). Super and Ultra have larger pools than
  Premium.
- **No published size.** Neither the pricing page nor the FAQ gives the pool as a number of messages,
  tokens or dollars. You can see your own use under Usage in the Grok dashboard.
- **It resets weekly.** If you use it up, it comes back at the weekly reset.

## What happens when the pool runs out

The Grok Bot page says that subscriptions "come with weekly usage included, with additional usage
billed based on token cost". The FAQ adds that prepaid credit covers use beyond the weekly pool, and
that you can leave Auto Top-up off or set a monthly limit under Additional usage credits.

What the sources don't say is the rate. There is no per-token price on either page.

On older accounts billed through Cursor, a user thread on the Cursor forum describes the order in
which usage is taken: the weekly allowance first, then referral or usage credits, then paid
on-demand usage. A Cursor staff member said in the
[thread](https://forum.cursor.com/t/grok-bot-gives-no-warning-before-weekly-usage-spills-into-paid-on-demand/169679)
that this was working as intended. Users there asked for a warning before paid use starts. The
workaround they describe is to watch the weekly meter and pause the bot near 100%.

The FAQ also warns that a spending cap doesn't stop a run that is already going, so a task can go
somewhat over it.

## Weekly limits were cut in October

Crypto Briefing reported on November 2, 2026 that many subscribers used up their weekly allowance
four or more days early in late October. xAI put some of it down to a billing dashboard glitch and
some to "necessary adjustments to how usage gets allocated". The same report says Elon Musk wrote on
November 1 that serving Grok Bot had become "unsustainably high relative to revenue"
([source](https://cryptobriefing.com/musk-grok-bot-usage-limits-high-costs/)). That is no promise of
a price change, but it is a reason not to plan around today's limits staying the same.

## What you really pay

Only the fixed part is known:

| Use | Grok Bot | What the sources let us say |
|---|---|---|
| Light | Premium, $30 a month, $360 over twelve months | Fixed fee, as long as you stay inside the pool |
| Steady | Super, $100 a month, $1,200 over twelve months | 3.5 times Premium's usage |
| Heavy | Ultra, $200 a month, $2,400 over twelve months | 12 times Premium's usage, then billed by token cost |

We can't give a dollar figure for what happens after the pool, because no rate is published. If you
go over, your bill is the plan price plus whatever your usage costs at token rates you can only see
on your own dashboard. Set a monthly limit first.

## Teams and Enterprise

The Grok Bot page has a Teams tab but lists no Teams prices. It says Grok Bot is available to teams
on the Teams plan and to Enterprise, and sends Enterprise buyers to "Contact sales". Per the FAQ,
every paid X subscription seat includes Grok Bot, and Enterprise admins turn it on. So team pricing
isn't published; you have to ask.

## Is there a free trial?

Some accounts see a Start Trial button. The FAQ says the trial is a usage credit with a 7-day
window, that it never turns into a paid plan, and that used credit isn't restored. Whether you see
it isn't something you can count on. See [Is Grok Bot free?](/compare/is-grok-bot-free).

## A cheaper way to get the same thing

[Casama](/pricing) is an open-source AI teammate app. Each bot has its own computer, and you can
watch it and take over. Its prices are all published:

| Option | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Casama Cloud Free** | $0 | $1 of credits a month, 3 bots, 1 computer with 5 hours |
| **Casama Cloud Plus** | $15 a month, or $150 a year | $5 of credits, 10 bots, 2 computers with 30 hours |
| **Casama Cloud Pro** | $49 a month, or $490 a year | $15 of credits, unlimited bots, 5 computers with 100 hours, 3 seats |
| **Casama Cloud Max** | $99 a month, or $899 a year | $30 of credits, unlimited bots, 10 computers with 300 hours, 10 seats |
| **Self-hosted** | $0 for the software | Any model, no limit on users, bots or computers |

On Casama Cloud a credit is a dollar. Credits pay for models, extra computer time and other paid
work, and every bot has a budget per run ($1 by default) and per day ($10 by default). When a run
reaches its budget it stops and tells you, and a bot that is waiting for your approval costs nothing.
Casama is also open about its limit: when credits run out, bots stop starting paid work, and nothing
is charged unless you buy a pack or change plan.

Self-hosting costs you a server and your model provider. With your own keys that is usually a few
dollars a month for light use, and local models cost nothing but run slower. See
[run Casama on your own server](/self-hosted).

Two honest caveats. Casama is new, first released in November 2026, and Grok Bot comes from a far
larger company. And choosing your own model is for self-hosted servers: on Casama Cloud, Casama
picks the model.

- You want native apps on every desktop and phone platform from a large vendor.

- You want to teach a workflow by recording it once.
- You already pay for an X Premium plan, so Grok Bot is included.

## Questions

### How much does Grok Bot cost?

Grok Bot costs $30 a month on Premium, $100 on Super and $200 on Ultra. Each plan includes weekly
usage, and extra use is billed by token cost. Teams and Enterprise prices aren't published.

### What is the Grok Bot weekly limit?

xAI doesn't publish it as a number. Super has 3.5 times the usage of Premium and Ultra has 12 times.
You can see your own use on the Usage page of the Grok dashboard.

### What happens if I go over the Grok Bot limit?

Use beyond the weekly pool is billed by token cost, drawn from prepaid credit. You can leave Auto
Top-up off or set a monthly limit, though a limit doesn't stop a run in progress.

### Is there a yearly Grok Bot price?

The Grok Bot page shows monthly prices only. Casama Cloud does have yearly prices: $150, $490 and
$899.

### What is the cheapest Grok Bot alternative?

Self-hosted Casama costs nothing for the software, and Casama Cloud's Free plan costs $0. See
[Grok Bot alternatives](/compare/grok-bot-alternatives), and [Grok Bot templates](/compare/grok-bot-templates)
for what you can start from.
