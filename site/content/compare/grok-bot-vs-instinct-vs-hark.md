## The short answer

- **Grok Bot** is for work. It has native apps on Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone and Android, team
  Bots in Slack and Teams, and plans at $30, $100 and $200 a month. There is no free plan.
- **Hark Pro** is for everyday life. Its free plan includes every feature, it says it doesn't
  train on your data, and it works from web and phone. There are no desktop apps or team plans.
- **Instinct** is for people who don't want an app at all. You text or call it. It is invite-only,
  with no published price.

## Three things to check before choosing

**Where the assistant works.** Grok Bot gives each person one cloud computer shared by all their
Bots. Hark gives each person a cloud browser you can watch. Instinct's own pages don't say.

**What it asks first.** Grok Bot uses an automatic check plus up to 20 rules you write. Hark says
it checks in before actions that need approval, without a published list. Instinct's terms promise
no confirmation steps.

**What happens to your data.** Hark says it doesn't train on your content. Grok Bot has a training
opt-out. Instinct trains by default unless you opt out.

## Where Casama fits

Casama is open source, so it answers the things none of these three can:

- **Run it yourself.** Free, on your own server, with your data on your machine.
- **Choose the model.** Any provider, a ChatGPT plan or local models when self-hosted.
- **A computer per bot.** Each bot has its own sandboxed Linux machine you can watch and take over.
- **Written rules.** What a bot must ask before doing is documented, and payments always ask.
- **Known costs.** Credits are dollars, with a budget per bot.
- **Open to everyone.** A free Cloud plan, with no invite and no region limits.

Casama is newer than all three and has a much smaller community.

## Questions

### Which is cheapest?

Hark Pro has a free plan, and Instinct is reported to be free during its beta. Grok Bot starts at
$30 a month. Casama has a free Cloud plan and is free to self-host.

### Which can I use outside the United States?

Grok Bot and Hark Pro don't publish a country list. Hark's memory is off in the EEA, the UK and
Switzerland. Instinct is invite-only. Casama has no region limits.

### Which is open source?

None of the three. Casama is, under AGPL-3.0.
