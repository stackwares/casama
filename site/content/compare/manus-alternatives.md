## Why people switch from Manus

Manus is a hosted agent with a cloud computer and a browser. It is good at finished work such as
slide decks, websites and wide research, with nothing to set up. Its sources also show where people
run into trouble:

- **Credits.** Credit use depends on how complex a task turns out to be. Trustpilot reviewers
  complain about credits lost to errors.
- **No choice of model.** Manus picks it on standard plans, and you can't host Manus yourself.
- **No approvals in the cloud.** None are described for its cloud agent. Its desktop app has three
  modes for commands on your own computer.
- **Access.** In August 2026 it deleted data and paused sales for users in regions it didn't name.

### Casama

- You want each bot on its own sandboxed Linux computer that you can watch and take over.
- You want risky steps, such as sending email, to wait for you from the start, and payments to
  always ask.
- You want to pay in dollars: on Casama Cloud a credit is a dollar, and each bot has a budget per
  run and per day.
- You want to self-host for free with any model, or start on a free hosted plan.

Worth knowing:

- It is new, first released in November 2026, with a small community.
- For a one-off deck or research report with nothing to set up, Manus is built for exactly that.
- Choosing your own model is for self-hosted servers. On Casama Cloud, Casama picks.

## Moving over from Manus

Manus projects keep instructions and files. To move one, make a bot for it, paste the project's
instructions, and add the files it needs. Re-create scheduled tasks as routines. Most products on
this list can run on a schedule, though limits differ.

## Questions

### What is the best free alternative to Manus?

It depends on where you live and how much you'll run it. Meta Muse is free for most use but only in
the United States. Hark Pro's free plan includes every feature, with a cap it doesn't publish.
Agent Zero is free software that you run yourself. Casama is free to self-host and has a free
hosted plan with $1 of credits and 5 computer hours a month.

### Is there an open-source alternative to Manus?

Yes. Agent Zero and Casama are open source and both give the agent a computer of its own. Our list
of the [best open-source AI agents](/compare/best-open-source-ai-agents) covers seven more.

### Which Manus alternative lets me choose the model?

Agent Zero, and Casama when you self-host. Claude Cowork uses Claude models only, ChatGPT Dots uses
OpenAI's model, and Grok Bot doesn't show which model ran. Lindy picks per task and lets you
choose per task. See [which AI agents let you choose the model](/learn/which-ai-agents-let-you-choose-the-model).

### Which Manus alternative asks before it acts?

Claude Duet asks before sending, paying, signing or deleting. Meta Muse needs approval for email,
moving data out and every purchase. Lindy waits for your OK on actions with outside impact. Casama
asks before risky steps by default and payments always ask. Agent Zero allows every tool until you
block it.

### Is there a Manus alternative that isn't priced in credits?

Claude Cowork and ChatGPT Dots are part of a subscription, though both have usage limits. Grok Bot
has a weekly usage pool. Agent Zero and self-hosted Casama cost nothing beyond your model provider.
On Casama Cloud, credits are dollars, so a task's cost reads as a price.
