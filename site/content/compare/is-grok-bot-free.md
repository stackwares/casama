## What the sources say

- **No free plan.** Grok Bot is included in the Premium, Super and Ultra X subscriptions. Plus and
  Free accounts don't include it ([Grok Bot FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq)).
- **A trial for some accounts.** Some accounts see a Start Trial button. The FAQ says the trial is a
  usage credit with a 7-day window, it never converts to a paid plan, the credit is used up by
  agent steps and tokens, and used credit isn't restored. If you cancel it you can't restart it.
- **"Get started for free".** The [Grok Bot page](https://x.ai/bot) has a button with that label, but
  gives no trial length or terms.
- **App Store trials.** Trials bought through Apple are run by Apple, and they turn into paid
  subscriptions unless you cancel first.

## What the cheapest plan gets you

Premium costs $30 a month. It includes weekly Grok Bot usage, but the amount isn't published. Super
at $100 has 3.5 times Premium's usage, and Ultra at $200 has 12 times. Beyond the weekly pool, usage
is billed by token cost. The details, and what is not published, are in
[Grok Bot pricing](/compare/grok-bot-pricing).

### What "free" leaves out

An agent has three costs: the software, the model and the computer. Open-source software removes the
first. The model is paid per use unless you run a local one, which is slower and weaker on long
tasks. The computer is free if you already own a machine to run it on. A free hosted plan, like
Casama's, covers a small allowance and then stops rather than charging you.

## Questions

### Is Grok Bot free to use?

No. It needs a paid X subscription: Premium at $30 a month, Super at $100 or Ultra at $200. Some
accounts get a 7-day trial credit.

### Does Grok Bot have a free trial?

Some accounts do. The FAQ says it is a usage credit with a 7-day window that never becomes a paid
plan. Not every account sees it, and the Grok Bot page gives no terms for its "Get started for free"
button.

### Is there a free Grok Bot alternative?

Yes. Casama's Cloud Free plan costs $0, and self-hosted Casama, OpenClaw, Agent Zero, NanoClaw and
nanobot are free software. Model usage costs money unless you use a local model.

### Is the Grok Bot free trial really free?

The credit is free, but Apple-run trials from the App Store become paid subscriptions unless you
cancel first, per the FAQ. Check which kind you have.

### Can I use Grok Bot without paying if I self-host it?

No. Grok Bot runs only in xAI's cloud, and its security docs say self-hosting isn't supported.
Casama can be self-hosted.
