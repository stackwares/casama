## What Grok Bot templates are

The [Grok Bot page](https://x.ai/bot) has a section called "Give each Bot a job" that lists eight
templates:

1. Sales Outbound
2. Talent Scout
3. Paid Media
4. Expense Manager
5. Product Performance
6. Bug Reproduction
7. Account Health
8. Chief of Staff

Only Sales Outbound has a description on the page. The page also links to "Explore more bots" in a
marketplace. The page gives no count, but x.ai's [sitemap](https://x.ai/sitemap.xml) listed 91
template pages when we checked on November 18, 2026.

You can also make your own. Per the [Grok Bot FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq), you open a Bot's
details, choose Share, then Create a copy, publish it and copy the link. Anyone with the link can
preview it on x.ai and add a copy. They don't get your computer, logins or history. The link shows
the Bot's configuration, so the FAQ says to remove secrets first, and a team can switch template
sharing off.

## How to start one in Casama

1. **A bot.** In the sidebar, select Add, then New bot, then pick a persona or Browse all. Starter
   bots from the gallery appear there. Change the instructions however you like.
2. **A routine.** Open the bot's profile, then Routines, then New routine, and tap one of the Ideas at
   the top. It fills in the name, instructions and schedule.
3. **A skill.** Go to Settings, then Skills, then Skill catalog, and select Install for one bot or
   the whole workspace.
4. **From a file or link.** Select Add, then Import a bot, and pick a template file or open a link.
   Casama shows what comes with it before you import. Importing a template never carries keys or
   sign-ins.

New bots start by asking before risky actions. Each bot gets its own computer. The full steps are in
the [bots guide](https://docs.casama.ai/guide/bots), and
[moving from Grok Bot to Casama](https://docs.casama.ai/guide/moving-from-grok-bot) maps Grok Bot's
ideas onto Casama's.

## Sharing your own

In Casama you open a bot's profile, then Advanced, then Share as Template. You can save it as a file
or copy a link, which works for 30 days. Memories are left out unless you tick them. You can also
add it to the public gallery by pull request to
[stackwares/casama-gallery](https://github.com/stackwares/casama-gallery).

## Where Grok Bot's catalogue is bigger

To be clear: Grok Bot has a bigger catalogue, about 91 templates against our 49 starter bots. It has a marketplace, and its templates are made by a large vendor and
set up for Slack and Teams team Bots. Casama's gallery is smaller in some areas, new, and made by
a small community. Casama's starter bots are starting points you will edit, not finished products. If one particular Grok Bot template is
what you want, check the marketplace for it first.

## Questions

### How many Grok Bot templates are there?

The Grok Bot page lists eight, and links to a marketplace with more. xAI doesn't state a total, but
its sitemap listed 91 template pages on November 18, 2026.

### What are the Grok Bot templates?

The eight on its page are Sales Outbound, Talent Scout, Paid Media, Expense Manager, Product
Performance, Bug Reproduction, Account Health and Chief of Staff.

### Are Grok Bot templates free?

The sources don't give a separate price for templates. They are used from a Grok Bot subscription,
which starts at $30 a month. See [Grok Bot pricing](/compare/grok-bot-pricing). Casama's gallery is
free, and every bot, routine and skill in it is open.

### Can I use my own template in Casama?

Not directly: there is no import from Grok Bot. You copy over the Bot's instructions and recreate
its schedules as routines. The [moving guide](https://docs.casama.ai/guide/moving-from-grok-bot)
shows how.

### Do Grok Bot templates share my logins?

No. Per the FAQ, someone who adds a copy doesn't receive your computer, logins or history. The link
does show the configuration, so remove secrets first.
