## Why people switch from Hermes Agent

Hermes Agent runs on your own machine by default, with Docker, SSH and cloud sandboxes as options,
and asks about shell commands that match risky patterns. Its strength is one agent that improves
with use. The alternatives change different parts of that:

- **More reach:** OpenClaw and nanobot connect to more chat apps.
- **Off your machine:** Agent Zero, NanoClaw, Rakazo and Casama keep the agent in a container or a
  computer of its own by default.
- **Asking first:** Casama and Rakazo ask before risky steps by default, not only for shell commands.
- **Several bots and people:** Casama and Rakazo have teams of bots and workspaces for several people.

## Questions

### What is the closest alternative to Hermes Agent?

OpenClaw: also free, MIT-licensed and on your own machine, with more chat apps. See
[OpenClaw vs Hermes Agent](/compare/openclaw-vs-hermes-agent).

### Is there a hosted Hermes Agent alternative?

Yes. Casama, Rakazo and MausBot have hosted plans, and Casama's has a free tier.

### Which alternatives keep the agent off my computer?

Agent Zero, NanoClaw, Rakazo and Casama, by default. OpenClaw, nanobot and MausBot run on your
machine unless you set up a sandbox.

### Can these alternatives use local models?

Most can, through Ollama or another OpenAI-style server. Check each product's sources below for the
details.
