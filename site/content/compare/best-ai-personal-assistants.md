## By what you need

### A free plan

Meta Muse is free for most use, but only in the United States, and a card is reportedly needed to
sign up. Hark Pro's free plan includes every feature, with a usage cap it doesn't publish. Manus
gives 300 credits a day. Casama Cloud's Free plan has $1 of credits, 3 bots and one cloud computer
with 5 hours a month. OpenClaw is free software, and you pay your model provider. Claude Duet and
ChatGPT Dots come with paid plans only.

### Nothing to install

Instinct is the one you text or call like a person, with a web workspace for settings. It is
invite or waitlist only, and its price isn't published. Meta Muse also works inside WhatsApp, and
Casama bots can be reached from Telegram, WhatsApp, iMessage, SMS, email and other chat apps.

### Errands on real websites

Booking, comparing and filling in forms need a browser. Hark Pro has its own cloud browser that you
can watch. Meta Muse and Casama give the assistant a cloud computer you can watch and take over.
ChatGPT Dots each have a cloud computer you can open. Claude Duet has one you can't watch. See
[what a computer use agent is](/learn/what-is-a-computer-use-agent).

### Asking before it acts

An assistant that books and pays needs firm stops. Claude Duet asks before sending, paying, signing
or deleting. Meta Muse sends email, outbound data and every purchase through a separate guard.
Casama asks before sending messages and other risky steps by default, and payments always ask.
Instinct's terms don't promise any confirmation step, and early testers reported an email sent
without permission. See [which AI agents ask before they act](/learn/which-ai-agents-ask-before-they-act).

### Privacy

Hark Pro says it doesn't train on your content, and Casama doesn't use your data for training.
Instinct and Meta Muse train by default until you opt out, and Claude's consumer plans do unless
you switch it off. OpenClaw has no cloud of its own, so your model provider's policy applies. See
[which AI agents don't train on your data](/learn/which-ai-agents-dont-train-on-your-data).

### Your own server

Only OpenClaw and Casama on this list can run on a machine you control. OpenClaw runs on your own
computer, with its sandbox and approvals off until you turn them on. Casama gives each bot its own
sandboxed computer on your server, and its phone apps reach that server from anywhere through
Casama Link.

## Where Casama fits, and where it doesn't

Casama suits someone who wants an assistant that keeps working when the laptop is closed, on a
computer of its own, with payments and messages waiting for approval, memory you can read and
edit, and apps on every phone and desktop.

It is new, first released in November 2026, with a small community. It reaches ten chat apps, fewer
than OpenClaw. On Casama Cloud you don't choose the model; you do when you self-host. If you want to
text an assistant with no app at all, Instinct is built for that, and if you want a free assistant
inside WhatsApp in the US, try Meta Muse.

## Questions

### What is the best free AI personal assistant?

In the US, Meta Muse has the most generous free plan for a full cloud-computer assistant. Outside
it, Hark Pro and Casama have free plans, and OpenClaw is free software you run yourself.

### Which AI personal assistant works on iPhone and Android?

Meta Muse, Hark Pro, Claude, ChatGPT, OpenClaw and Casama all have phone apps. Manus lists an iPhone
app. See [which AI agents have iPhone and Android apps](/learn/which-ai-agents-have-iphone-and-android-apps).

### Can an AI personal assistant work while I'm away?

Yes, if it runs somewhere other than your phone or laptop. Hosted assistants keep working in their
cloud. A self-hosted one needs a machine that stays on. Most can also run on a schedule; see
[which AI agents run tasks on a schedule](/learn/which-ai-agents-run-tasks-on-a-schedule).

### What does an AI personal assistant cost?

From nothing, on a free plan or with free software and a local model, to $20 to $200 a month for
most paid plans here. See [how much an AI agent costs](/learn/how-much-does-an-ai-agent-cost).
