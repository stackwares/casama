## Why people switch from Grok Bot

Grok Bot has native apps on every desktop and phone platform and team Bots in Slack and Teams. Its
sources also show the reasons people look elsewhere:

- **No free plan.** Paid plans are $30, $100 or $200 a month, with a weekly usage pool and then
  pay per use. Subscribers reported weekly limits being cut in late October 2026 without warning.
- **One computer per person.** All of a person's Bots share one computer, its files and its
  sign-ins.
- **No choice of model.** You can't choose the model, and you aren't shown which one ran.
- **No self-hosting.** It runs in xAI's cloud only, on computers in the United States.

## Moving over from Grok Bot

Each Grok Bot becomes a Casama bot: paste its instructions, re-create its schedules as routines and
sign in to the apps it used. Each bot gets its own computer, so sign-ins no longer leak from one
bot to another. Team Bots in Slack or Teams reconnect per bot.

## Questions

### What is the best open-source alternative to Grok Bot?

Rakazo is the closest in shape: a computer per bot, hosted or self-hosted. MausBot suits people who
want to reuse subscriptions they already pay for. Casama adds approvals on by default and native
phone apps. Agent Zero is the most hackable, but you run it yourself.

### Is there a free alternative to Grok Bot?

Yes. Agent Zero is free software. Rakazo and Casama are free to self-host, and MausBot's desktop app
is free. Manus gives 300 credits a day, and Casama Cloud's Free plan has $1 of credits and 5
computer hours a month.

### Which Grok Bot alternative lets me choose the model?

Rakazo, MausBot, OpenBot and Agent Zero all take your own keys or subscriptions, and so does Casama
when self-hosted. Manus, ChatGPT Dots and Claude Cowork pick the model for you.

### Can I self-host a Grok Bot alternative?

Yes: Rakazo, MausBot, OpenBot, Agent Zero and Casama can all run on your own server. See
[how to self-host an AI agent](/learn/how-to-self-host-an-ai-agent).

### Which has apps on every platform?

Grok Bot, Rakazo and Casama list apps for Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone and Android. See
[which AI agents have iPhone and Android apps](/learn/which-ai-agents-have-iphone-and-android-apps).
