## The short answer

- **Rakazo** runs on your own server, as a desktop app, or on its cloud. Its hosted plans let you
  bring your own keys or a ChatGPT plan, or buy credits at the provider's price.
- **Casama** runs on your own server, Mac or Linux computer, or on Casama Cloud. Its hosted plans
  include dollar credits, and there is a free one that doesn't end.
- The choice is about details, so the table matters more than this summary. We make Casama, so
  weigh our view accordingly.

## Where Rakazo wins

{{best-at: rakazo}}

Model choice on the hosted version is the clearest difference. On Rakazo's cloud you can use your
own keys or a ChatGPT plan. On Casama Cloud, Casama picks the model.

## Where Casama wins

- **A free hosted plan.** Casama Cloud's Free plan has $1 of credits a month, 3 bots and one cloud
  computer with 5 hours. Rakazo's hosted plans have a 7-day trial.
- **Chat apps.** Casama lists ten: Telegram, email, SMS, Slack, WhatsApp, Messenger, Feishu,
  iMessage, Microsoft Teams and Discord. Rakazo lists Slack and Teams, with iMessage, WhatsApp and
  Telegram only when self-hosted.
- **Roles for people.** Casama workspaces have owner, admin and member roles, and extra seats on
  paid plans. Rakazo has organizations, spaces and group chats, and its pages don't describe roles.
- **Approval rules you write.** Casama lets you set rules per kind of action, such as "send email
  to my own company without asking". Payments always ask. A checkpoint is saved before a command
  that deletes data.
- **A stated position on training.** Casama says your data is not used to train models. Rakazo's
  privacy page doesn't say either way.

## Where Casama is weaker

- It is newer still: first released in November 2026. Rakazo's repository dates from August 2026
  and has about 9,000 GitHub stars.
- No model choice on the hosted version, as above.
- Hosted model usage comes out of the credits in your plan. Rakazo sells credits at the provider's
  price with no markup.
- A wide choice of sandbox providers for self-hosting is something Rakazo leads with. Casama's
  default is Docker.

## Three questions to decide

1. **Do you want to choose the model on a hosted plan?** If yes, Rakazo. If you would rather not
   think about models, Casama Cloud picks for you, and self-hosted Casama takes any provider.
2. **Do you want to start hosted for free?** Casama has a free plan. Rakazo has a trial.
3. **Which chat apps do you need without running a server?** If Slack or Teams is enough, either
   works. If you want Telegram or WhatsApp, compare what each offers on the version you plan to
   use.

## What moving costs

Less than between most products, because the ideas map one to one: bots, instructions, memory,
routines and a computer per bot.

**From Rakazo to Casama.** Recreate each bot, paste its instructions, and re-add its routines and
app connections. On a self-hosted server, add your model key again. Files on a bot's computer need
copying across.

**From Casama to Rakazo.** The same steps in reverse. Your Casama workspace exports in full. If you
used workspace roles or action rules, check what Rakazo offers in their place before you move a
team.

Self-hosting either one is free, so you can run both for a week on the same job.

## Questions

### Are both open source?

Yes. Rakazo is Apache-2.0 and Casama is AGPL-3.0, with its SDKs under Apache-2.0. Casama keeps enterprise features in a separate `ee/` folder with its own
licence.

### Which is cheaper?

Self-hosted, both are free. Hosted, the plan prices match at $15, $49 and $99 a month. Casama adds
a free plan and includes credits. Rakazo adds credits from $20 at the provider's price.

### Which has more computer time?

Rakazo's entry hosted plan has 25 computer hours a month. Casama's Plus plan has 30, and its Free
plan has 5.

### Do both have phone apps?

Yes. Both list iPhone, iPad and Android apps, along with desktop and web.
