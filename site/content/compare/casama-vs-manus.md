## The short answer

- **Manus** is a closed, hosted agent. It works in a cloud sandbox with a browser, and a desktop
  app can run commands on your computer. Manus picks the model. Plans are priced in credits.
- **Casama** is open source under AGPL-3.0. Each bot has its own sandboxed Linux computer, asks
  before risky steps, and keeps memory you can read and edit. You can self-host it free or use
  Casama Cloud.
- We make Casama, so read the table and the sources and weigh our view accordingly.

## Where Casama wins

- **You can run it yourself.** Casama's code is open and self-hosting is free, with no limit on
  users or bots. Manus is closed and runs in its own cloud.
- **Approvals.** Under the default setting, a Casama bot asks before sending email or chat
  messages, making a page public and other high-risk actions. Payments always ask. Manus's pages
  describe no approvals for its cloud, and three modes when it works on your own computer.
- **Model choice when self-hosted.** Any provider, a ChatGPT plan or local models. Manus gives no
  choice on standard plans.
- **Chat apps.** Casama has ten. Manus lists Slack and email.
- **Memory.** Each Casama bot keeps memory you can read and edit. Manus projects keep instructions
  and files, and no separate memory is described.
- **Spending you can predict.** On Casama Cloud a credit is a dollar, each bot has a budget per run
  and per day, and each reply shows what it cost.

## Where Casama is weaker

- It is new: first released in November 2026, with a small community.
- Manus's strength is the finished deliverable. If a polished slide deck is the whole job, try
  Manus first.
- On Casama Cloud you don't choose the model either. Choice comes with self-hosting.
- Self-hosting is work. Manus needs no setup at all.

## Three questions to decide

1. **Does it matter who runs it?** If your data must stay on a machine you control, or you want to
   read the code, only Casama fits. If hosted by the vendor is fine, both do.
2. **Do you want one-off deliverables or standing jobs?** For "make me this deck", Manus is built
   for it. For a support bot or a research bot that works every day and remembers how you like
   things, Casama is built for it.
3. **How much should it ask?** If it will send mail or spend money, check how each product
   handles that before you connect anything.

## What moving costs

**From Manus to Casama.** We haven't confirmed an export tool on Manus's side, so plan to copy by
hand. Take each project's instructions and files, create a bot for each standing job, and paste
the instructions in. Recreate scheduled tasks as routines. Unused Manus credits don't come with
you.

**From Casama to Manus.** Your workspace exports in full. Instructions paste across. You give up
self-hosting, per-bot computers and the approval rules you wrote.

Both have a free way to start: Manus gives 300 credits a day, and Casama Cloud's Free plan has $1
of credits a month. Try the same task on each before paying.

## Questions

### Is Manus open source?

No. Manus is closed. Casama is open source under AGPL-3.0, with a separate `ee/` folder under its
own licence.

### Which is cheaper?

It depends on the work. Manus Pro starts at $20 a month for 4,000 credits, and credit use depends
on how complex a task turns out to be. Casama Cloud is free, $15, $49 or $99 a month with dollar
credits included, and self-hosting is free.

### Is Manus available everywhere?

Manus says it paused sales in some regions in August 2026. Check its help pages for your country.
Casama is open to everyone.

### Can Casama make slide decks and websites?

A Casama bot can make documents, pages and sites on its computer and keep them in the Library. We
make no claim that the results match Manus's.
