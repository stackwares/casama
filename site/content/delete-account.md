## Before you delete

- **Cancel any paid plan first.** Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription bought in the
  App Store or Google Play, because Apple and Google bill it. Cancel it in **Settings → Apple ID →
  Subscriptions** on your iPhone, or in **Play Store → Payments & subscriptions** on Android. For a
  plan bought on the web or in the desktop app, open **Settings → Billing → Manage Subscription**.
- **Keep a copy if you want one.** Open **Settings → Privacy → Export My Data** for a ZIP of
  everything you can see. Download files and pages you want to keep from the Library.
- **Shared workspaces.** A workspace where you are the only owner is deleted with your account,
  even if other people use it. If it should stay, [write to us](mailto:support@casama.ai) first.

## Delete it in the app

This works the same in the iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows and Linux apps, and in the web app
at [app.casama.ai](https://app.casama.ai):

1. Sign in and open **Settings → Privacy**.
2. Under **Danger Zone**, tap **Delete My Account**.
3. Type your email address to confirm.

You are signed out everywhere at once, and the app shows the date your data will be erased. From
the command line, `casama account delete --confirm you@example.com` does the same.

## Delete it without the app

If you no longer have the app or can't sign in, email
[privacy@casama.ai](mailto:privacy@casama.ai) from the address on your Casama account, with the
subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm it's you, then delete the account the same
way and tell you when it's done. We aim to reply within 30 days.

## What is deleted, and when

| What | When |
|---|---|
| Your sign-ins on every device, push notifications to your devices, and device data you shared (calendar, health summaries, places and the like) | Right away |
| Your account and profile | After a 7-day grace period |
| Every workspace where you are the only owner, with its bots, chats, memories, files, pages, routines and computers (including computer snapshots) | After the grace period |
| Your membership in workspaces that have another owner | After the grace period; those workspaces and their data stay |
| Your usage analytics | With your account |
| Encrypted backup copies | 14 days after the account is erased |

## What we keep

- **Billing records**, such as invoices and payment records, for as long as tax and accounting laws
  require.
- Questions you asked Casama Help on our websites are never tied to you, so there is nothing to
  delete there.

Deleting your account doesn't delete what other people copied or downloaded from links you shared,
and doesn't stop a store subscription you didn't cancel.

## Self-hosted servers

If you use a Casama server that you or someone else runs, your data is on that server, not with
us. Delete your account in the app the same way, or ask whoever runs the server. See
[Privacy and security](https://docs.casama.ai/guide/privacy#delete-your-account) in the docs and
our [privacy policy](/privacy) for the details.
