## Side by side

| | Chatbot | AI agent |
|---|---|---|
| What you get | An answer or a draft | A finished task |
| Who does the clicking | You | The agent |
| Tools | Usually none, or a web search | Browser, files, shell, your connected apps |
| Where it works | Inside the chat | On a computer: yours, or one of its own |
| Length of a task | One reply | Many steps, minutes to hours |
| When it works | When you type | Also on schedules and triggers |
| Memory | The current chat | Facts and preferences kept across chats |
| Risk | It can be wrong | It can be wrong and act on it |
| Cost | Per message | Per step, so longer tasks cost more |

## The same request, twice

**"Find three hotels in Lisbon under €150 for the first weekend of May, near the tram."**

A chatbot gives you a list from what it knows, which may be out of date, and you check each one.

An agent opens the booking sites, sets the dates, filters by price, checks the map, and returns
three options with live prices and links. If you say "book the second one", it fills in the form
and stops for your approval before paying.

## When a chatbot is enough

- You want an explanation, an idea or a first draft.
- The task is one step.
- Nothing needs to be looked up live or done in another app.

## When you want an agent

- The task has several steps across sites or apps.
- It repeats: every morning, every Monday, every time an email arrives.
- It should carry on while you do something else.
- It needs to remember how you like things done.

## What changes when software can act

Because an agent acts, the questions to ask are different from the ones you'd ask of a chatbot:

- **What can it touch?** An agent on its own sandboxed computer can't reach your files. One that
  runs on your laptop can.
- **What does it ask first?** Look for approvals before sending, paying and deleting.
- **Can you see what it did?** A step-by-step record lets you check its work.
- **Can you undo it?** Checkpoints or backups turn a mistake into an inconvenience.
- **What stops a runaway task?** Budgets and limits on repeated failures.
