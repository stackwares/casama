## From agent to teammate

"AI agent" describes how the software works: a model that uses tools in a loop. "AI teammate"
describes how you work with it. The difference is what carries over from one task to the next:

| | One-off AI agent | AI teammate |
|---|---|---|
| Lifetime | One task, then gone | Stays for months |
| Role | Whatever you ask this time | A standing job: assistant, researcher, support |
| Memory | Starts fresh | Remembers preferences, people and past work |
| Initiative | Waits for a request | Runs on schedules, reacts to events, suggests work |
| Workspace | Borrowed for the task | Its own computer, with its files where it left them |
| Working with people | One person, one chat | Group chats, shared spaces, handing work to others |

## What makes one useful

- **A clear job.** "Research" or "customer support" works better than "everything".
- **Memory you can check.** A teammate that remembers the wrong thing needs correcting, so you
  should be able to read and edit what it knows.
- **Somewhere to keep its work.** Files, sign-ins and half-finished tasks should still be there
  tomorrow.
- **Ways to reach you.** A phone notification, a chat app, an email: wherever you already are.
- **Limits.** What it may do alone, what it must ask about, and how much it may spend.

## A day with an AI teammate

A personal assistant bot might:

1. Send a morning brief at 7:30 with today's meetings and the three emails that need you.
2. Notice a meeting clash and suggest a new time, with a button to do it.
3. Draft replies to routine emails and wait for your approval before sending.
4. Book the dinner you mentioned yesterday, because it remembered the restaurant you like.
5. Write up what it did, with the cost.

None of that needed a prompt after the first week.

## Who offers AI teammates

Several products now use the idea: hosted ones from the large AI labs, and open-source ones you can
run yourself. They differ most in where the teammate works (its own computer or yours), what it
asks before doing, which models it can use, and whether you can host it. Our
[comparison pages](/alternatives/) put them side by side.

## AI teammates in Casama

Casama calls its teammates bots. Each one has instructions, a model, memory, skills, its own
sandboxed Linux computer and limits you set. Bots send check-ins and suggestions, run routines on
schedules and triggers, work in group chats with people and other bots, and reach you in the app,
by push notification or in chat apps such as Slack and Telegram.
