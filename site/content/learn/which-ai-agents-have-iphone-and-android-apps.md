## Does it matter if the agent has no phone app?

Less than you might think, for talking to it. Most agents that run on your own machine reach you
through a chat app you already have, and a message in Telegram or WhatsApp works anywhere.

A phone app matters for three other things:

1. **Approvals.** If the agent asks before risky steps, you want to answer from your lock screen,
   not find a laptop. A push notification with Approve and Deny is faster than a chat reply.
2. **Watching its screen.** Agents with a computer of their own can show it live. That needs an
   app, or at least a phone-sized web page.
3. **Your own server.** A self-hosted agent sits on your network. A phone app that can reach it
   from outside, without you opening ports, is hard to replace with a chat app.

## What to check in any phone app

| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| Is it in the App Store and Google Play? | Apps handed out by link or by invite are harder to keep updated |
| Can I approve or deny from a notification? | Otherwise work waits until you open the app |
| Can I watch or take over the agent's computer? | You'll want to when a site needs a sign-in |
| Does it work with my own server? | Some apps only talk to the vendor's cloud |
| Is it the full product or a remote control? | Some phone apps only chat; settings live on the desktop |

## Questions

### Which open-source AI agents have phone apps?

OpenClaw, Rakazo and Casama list apps for both iPhone and Android. Hermes Agent, Agent Zero,
NanoClaw and nanobot don't have one; you reach them through chat apps or a web page.

### What is the best AI assistant app for iPhone?

For an assistant that acts, not just answers, look at what it can do from the phone: approvals,
watching its screen, and reaching it when you're away. Our list of the
[best AI personal assistants](/compare/best-ai-personal-assistants) compares eight.

### Can I use an AI agent from my phone without a phone app?

Yes. Many agents connect to Telegram, WhatsApp, Signal, iMessage or SMS, and you message them like
a person. Instinct is built around texts and calls alone.
