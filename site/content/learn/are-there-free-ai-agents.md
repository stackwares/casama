## Three things an agent costs

| Cost | What it is | When it's free |
|---|---|---|
| The software | The agent itself | Open-source agents; free plans of hosted ones |
| The model | Each step is a call to an AI model | Local models; free credits; small free allowances |
| The computer | Somewhere for the agent to work | Your own machine; included hours on a free plan |

An agent is only free for you if all three are.

## Free option 1: open source on your own machine

Install an open-source agent on a computer you already own. The software costs nothing and so does
the computer.

- **With a local model**, the whole thing is free. Expect it to be slower and weaker on long tasks
  than the best hosted models.
- **With your own API key**, you pay the model provider for what you use. Light use is often a few
  dollars a month.
- **With a subscription you already have**, such as a ChatGPT plan, some agents can sign in with it
  so there is no new bill.

Our [comparison of open-source AI agents](/compare/best-open-source-ai-agents) lists which do what.

## Free option 2: a hosted free plan

Some hosted agents have a free tier. Read what it includes:

- **How much usage.** Often a small monthly amount of credits or tasks.
- **Whether a card is needed** to sign up.
- **What happens at the limit.** It should stop, not start charging.
- **Whether your data is used for training** on the free plan.

## What "free" usually leaves out

- Heavy use. An agent that works for hours a day will pass any free allowance.
- The strongest models.
- Team features.
- Live video of the agent's screen, long-running computers, large storage.

## Questions

### What is the cheapest way to run an AI agent all day?

Your own always-on machine, an open-source agent, and a local model for routine work, with a
hosted model on your own key for the hard tasks.

### Do free plans include an agent with its own computer?

Some do, with limited hours. Casama's Free plan includes one cloud computer for 5 hours a month.

### Is a free AI agent safe?

Price doesn't tell you. Check what it asks before doing, and what happens to your data.
