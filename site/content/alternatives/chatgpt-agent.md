## How they differ

A dot is one agent tied to your ChatGPT account on OpenAI's most expensive plans. Casama gives you several bots, each with a job and its own computer, on a free plan or your own server. We could not read OpenAI's own pages for this comparison, so its column rests on press coverage; check the sources.

## Moving from ChatGPT Dots to Casama

Recreate your dot as a Casama bot: paste its role into the bot's instructions, reconnect apps, and add routines. If you self-host and have a ChatGPT plan, a Casama bot can sign in with it and use it for chat and tools.

Start on [Casama Cloud's free plan](https://app.casama.ai), or

## Questions

### What happened to ChatGPT agent?

Reports say the older ChatGPT agent mode was replaced by ChatGPT Work for long tasks, with dots as the always-on agent. We could not confirm this on OpenAI's own pages.

### Are ChatGPT dots free?

No. Reports say a dot is included only with Pro and Business Premium.

### Can I use my ChatGPT plan with Casama?

Yes, when you self-host: sign in with your ChatGPT plan instead of a key.

### Is Casama available in Europe?

Yes. Casama has no region limits.
