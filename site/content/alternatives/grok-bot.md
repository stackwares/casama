## How they differ

Grok Bot is a closed, hosted product from a large lab, sold as part of a subscription with a weekly usage pool. Casama is open source. You can run it on your own server for free with any model, or use Casama Cloud, where credits are dollars and each bot has budgets you set.

## Questions

### Is Grok Bot free?

No. Its docs say it is included in paid plans only, with a 7-day trial credit for some accounts. Casama has a free Cloud plan, and self-hosting is free.

### How much does Grok Bot cost?

Plans are $30, $100 and $200 a month, with usage from a weekly pool and pay-per-use beyond it. The size of the pool isn't published.

### Can I self-host Grok Bot?

No. Its security docs say self-hosting isn't supported. Casama is free to self-host.

### Does Casama work in Slack and Teams?

Yes, along with Telegram, WhatsApp, iMessage, Discord and others.
