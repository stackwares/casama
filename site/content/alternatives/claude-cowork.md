## How they differ

Cowork and Duet are the right choice if you live in Claude already. Casama is a separate, open product: a team of bots with their own computers, memory and routines, reachable from native apps and chat apps, on Casama Cloud or your own server.

## Choose Casama if

- You want to choose the model, or mix several.
- You want to watch and take over a bot's computer. Duet's browser can't be watched.
- You are outside the United States, where Duet isn't offered.
- You want a free plan, or to self-host for nothing.
- You want bots reachable in Slack, Telegram, WhatsApp or iMessage.

## Moving from Claude Cowork and Duet to Casama

Recreate recurring Cowork tasks as Casama routines and move your instructions into a bot. Connect the same apps. If you have an Anthropic API key, a self-hosted Casama bot can run on Claude models.

Start on [Casama Cloud's free plan](https://app.casama.ai), or

## Questions

### Is Claude Cowork free?

No. Anthropic's pages list it on paid plans only, and Duet isn't on the Free plan.

### Can Casama use Claude models?

Yes. When you self-host, add an Anthropic key and pick a Claude model per bot.

### What is the difference between Cowork and Duet?

Cowork is an agent mode for tasks you start. Duet is an always-on assistant with its own cloud computer, in a US-only preview.

### Does my Claude plan limit apply to Casama?

No. Casama Cloud uses credits, and self-hosted Casama uses your own model key.
