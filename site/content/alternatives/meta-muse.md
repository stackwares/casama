## How they differ

Muse is a consumer product from Meta with a documented safety design and a large free allowance. Casama is open source: you can read how it decides what to ask, run it on your own machine, and choose the model.

## Choose Casama if

- You are outside the United States.
- You don't want your interactions used for training by default.
- You want your data on a server you run.
- You want to choose the model, or run a local one.
- You want desktop apps for Mac, Windows and Linux, and more chat apps than WhatsApp.

## Questions

### Is Meta Muse free?

Meta says it is free for most needs, with paid plans for heavier use. Press reports the paid plans at $20 and $100 a month.

### Is Meta Muse available outside the US?

Not at launch. Casama has no region limits.

### Does Meta Muse train on my data?

Meta's pages say interactions can be used for training by default, and that you can turn it off in settings. Casama doesn't use your data to train models.

### Can I message a Casama bot on WhatsApp?

Yes.
