## How they differ

Hark is a polished phone-first assistant for everyday errands. Casama is a team of bots for work and life, with a full Linux computer for each, desktop apps, group chats with other people, and published limits.

## Choose Casama if

- You want published limits: credits in dollars and a budget per bot.
- You want desktop apps for Mac, Windows and Linux.
- You want to share bots with a team.
- You want a full computer for each bot (shell, files, browser), not only a browser.
- You want to self-host, or to choose the model.

## Moving from Hark Pro to Casama

In Casama, choose **Paste a Bot's Setup** and pick Hark Pro. Send Hark the prompt and paste its reply: what it does for you, its scheduled tasks and what it remembers come over as a bot, routines and memories, and you review each one first. Then connect the same accounts (mail, calendar, Slack).

Start on [Casama Cloud's free plan](https://app.casama.ai), or
[run Casama on your own server](/self-hosted).

## Questions

### Is Hark free?

Yes. Its pricing page lists a free plan with every feature; paid plans at $20 and $100 a month raise usage.

### Does Hark train on my data?

Its pages say it doesn't train AI models on your account data. Casama doesn't either.

### Does Hark have a desktop app?

None is listed. Casama has apps for Mac, Windows and Linux.

### Is memory available in Europe?

Hark's pages say memory is off in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. Casama's memory works everywhere.
