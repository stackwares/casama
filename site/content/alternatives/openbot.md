## How they differ

OpenBot's centre is company control over what AI coworkers may do, deployed inside your own cloud. Casama's centre is the bot itself: its own computer, memory, routines and approvals, for one person or a team, with phone and desktop apps that are generally available.

## Questions

### Is OpenBot open source?

The OpenBot app is MIT-licensed. It needs CopilotKit Intelligence to run, which has a free licence for personal laptop use and a paid one for production.

### Which OpenBot is this?

CopilotKit's, at open.bot. Other products share the name.

### Is OpenBot free?

For one person on their own laptop, yes. Its Business edition costs up to $29 per active user a month.

### Does Casama have single sign-on?

Yes, with audit export. Some enterprise features live in a separately licensed `ee/` folder; the rest is open source (AGPL-3.0, with the SDKs under Apache-2.0).
