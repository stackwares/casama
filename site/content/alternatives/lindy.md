## How they differ

Lindy is built around work chat. You @mention it in Slack channels, text it, or use it in Gmail, and
it acts across your connected tools, waiting for your OK before anything with outside impact. It is
a hosted service only.

Casama is built around bots with their own computers. Each bot has a sandboxed Linux machine with a
shell, files and a browser, so it can install software, write code, build files and browse, not only
call integrations. Approvals work in a similar spirit. The big differences are ownership and price:
Casama is open source, can run on your own server with any model, and has a free hosted plan with no
end date.

## Moving from Lindy to Casama

Recreate each routine as a Casama routine with the same schedule or trigger, and give a bot the
instructions your Lindy follows. Connect the same apps (Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack and others) in
Connections. Paste anything important from Lindy's memory files into the bot's memories.

Start on [Casama Cloud's free plan](https://app.casama.ai), or

## Questions

### Is Lindy open source?

No. Lindy is a hosted service and offers no source code or self-hosting. Casama is open source under
AGPL-3.0.

### Is Lindy free?

Its free plan is $50 of credits that last seven days. After that, plans start at $29.99 a month per
seat.

### Does Lindy train on my data?

Lindy says neither it nor its providers use your data to train AI models.

### Can Casama work in Slack like Lindy?

Yes. Casama bots can join Slack, as well as Teams, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp and iMessage.
