## How they differ

If you are choosing between these two, the choice is about details, so read the table. In short: Rakazo's hosted plans bring your own model account or sell credits at the provider's price, with larger computers. Casama's hosted plans include dollar credits and seats, connect Telegram, WhatsApp and other chat apps on Cloud too, and add checkpoints, budgets and workspace roles.

- You want a free hosted plan. Rakazo's hosted plans start with a 7-day trial.

## Questions

### Is Rakazo open source?

Yes, under Apache-2.0. Casama is open source too, under AGPL-3.0.

### Which is cheaper?

Self-hosting either is free. Hosted plans cost the same. Rakazo adds no markup on model usage bought as credits; Casama includes credits in every plan.

### Which has bigger cloud computers?

Rakazo's pricing page lists larger machines per plan. Casama's plans include more seats and a free tier.

### Do both have phone apps?

Yes, both list iPhone and Android apps.
