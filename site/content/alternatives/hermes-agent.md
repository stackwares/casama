## How they differ

Hermes Agent is built around one agent that gets better the longer you use it, with a wide choice of models and of places for its commands to run. Casama is built around several bots that each hold a job, each on its own sandboxed computer, with apps on phone and desktop and rules for what every kind of action must ask before doing.

- You want native iPhone and Android apps with push notifications today.

- You want each bot on its own managed computer you can watch and take over.
- Several people will share it, with accounts and roles.
- You want a hosted plan where your data isn't used for training.

## Moving from Hermes Agent to Casama

Recreate each Hermes setup as a Casama bot: paste its instructions, add the same model key, and turn its scheduled jobs into routines. Skills are plain instructions in both, so the useful ones can be copied across as Casama skills.

## Questions

### Is Hermes Agent free?

The agent is free and MIT-licensed. The optional Nous Portal has plans at $0, $20, $100 and $200 a month. Self-hosted Casama is free too.

### Hermes Agent or OpenClaw?

See our [OpenClaw vs Hermes Agent comparison](/compare/openclaw-vs-hermes-agent).

### Does Hermes Agent have a mobile app?

Not yet; its pages describe one as coming. Casama has apps for iPhone, iPad and Android.

### Can both use local models?

Yes, through Ollama and similar servers.
