## How they differ

Instinct's appeal is that there is nothing to learn: you message it like a person. Its maker publishes very little about how it works, so much of its column comes from reviews. Casama publishes everything, down to the code that decides when a bot must ask.

## Moving from Instinct to Casama

Instinct has no export that we could find, so Casama asks Instinct instead: in Casama, choose **Paste a Bot's Setup**, text Instinct the prompt, and paste its reply. Its instructions, regular check-ins and what it knows about you come over, and you review each one first. Then connect your mail and calendar.

## Questions

### How do I get access to Instinct?

Its site offers a waitlist and invites from existing users. Casama is open to everyone.

### How much does Instinct cost?

No prices are published. Press reports it has been free during the invite-only beta.

### Can I text a Casama bot?

Yes: SMS, iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram and other chat apps can be connected to a bot, and bots can take live calls in the app.

### Is Instinct open source?

No.
