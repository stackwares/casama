## How they differ

Manus is strongest when you hand it one big task and want a finished deliverable back. Casama is built for bots that stay: each has a role, a memory, routines and its own computer, and stops for your approval before sending, paying or deleting.

- You want to run it on your own server, with your own model keys or local models.
- You want risky steps to wait for approval in the cloud too.
- You want costs in dollars with a budget per bot, per run and per day.
- You want standing teammates with memory and check-ins, not only one-off tasks.

## Moving from Manus to Casama

There is no import. Recreate your recurring Manus automations as Casama routines, and move project instructions into a bot's instructions or a skill. Files a bot makes land in Casama's Library.

Start on [Casama Cloud's free plan](https://app.casama.ai), or

## Questions

### Is there an open-source Manus alternative?

Yes. Casama is open source under AGPL-3.0 and free to self-host. OpenClaw and Hermes Agent are open source too, and run on your own machine.

### How much does Manus cost?

Its help pages list a free plan with 300 credits a day and Pro from $20 a month for 4,000 credits, up to $200. Tasks spend credits according to their complexity.

### Does Manus ask before acting?

In the cloud, no approval step is described; it asks you to take over for sign-ins. Its desktop app has approval modes for actions on your own computer.

### Can Casama make slide decks and documents?

Bots have office tools on their computers and can produce documents and PDFs. Manus's built-in deliverable tools are more polished.
