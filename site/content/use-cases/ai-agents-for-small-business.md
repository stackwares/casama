## What the bot does

- **Answers customers** from your help documents, around the clock.
- **Does the admin**: invoices, forms, data entry, chasing late payments.
- **Researches**: suppliers, competitors, prices, with sources.
- **Keeps the books tidy**: collects receipts, matches them, flags what's missing.
- **Reports**: a Monday summary of sales, support and what needs a decision.

## Where it stops to ask

- Refunds, payments and anything else that costs money.
- Messages to customers, until you allow routine ones.
- Anything outside the limits you set for that bot.

You set these rules per bot and per action. See [how approvals work](/security).

## What it looks like

1. Kelly, the support bot, answers 23 emails overnight. One $48 refund waits for the owner's OK.
2. Theo, the research bot, sends Monday's supplier price check with every source cited.
3. A new hire is added to the workspace and can use both bots, but can't change their rules.

## Why its own computer matters

Each Casama bot works on a sandboxed Linux machine of its own, with a browser, files and a shell.
It keeps going when you close your laptop, it can't reach your own files, and you can watch its
screen or take over at any time. Before a command that deletes data, its computer is saved, so the
work can be undone.

## Set it up

1. Create a workspace and invite your team. Pro includes 3 seats and Max includes 10.
2. Add one bot per job, from the built-in personas (Support, Sales, Ops, Researcher and others) or your own instructions.
3. Give each bot only the apps it needs.
4. Set a budget per run and per day for each bot. The Usage screen shows spend by bot and by person.

Ready-made skills, starter bots and routines are in the [gallery](https://gallery.casama.ai).

## Questions

### How much does it cost for a team?

Pro is $49 a month with 3 seats, unlimited bots and $15 of credits. Extra people are $12 each. Self-hosting is free for any number of people.

### Can a bot spend money without approval?

No. Payments always ask.

### Do we need an IT person?

Not for Casama Cloud. Self-hosting needs someone comfortable with Docker.
