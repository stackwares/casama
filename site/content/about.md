## What Casama is

Casama gives you AI teammates, called bots. Each bot has its own instructions, memory, skills and
a sandboxed Linux computer. It can research, write, code, browse and run tasks on a schedule, and it
asks before doing anything risky. You reach your bots from apps on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac,
Windows, Linux and the web.

You can run Casama on your own server for free, with any AI model, or use Casama Cloud, which has a
free plan and paid plans from $15 a month.

This Casama is the AI app at casama.ai. It is not related to other companies or products with the
same name.

## Press kit

Writing about Casama? Use these.

**One sentence.** Casama is an open-source app for AI teammates that each have their own computer,
remember how you work and ask before anything risky.

**One paragraph.** Casama gives people AI teammates, called bots, that do real work on computers of
their own: they browse, fill in forms, write files and run code on a sandboxed Linux machine you
can watch and take over. Bots remember what matters, start on schedules and triggers, and stop for
your approval before risky steps such as sending, paying or deleting. Casama is open source under
AGPL-3.0 and free to self-host with any AI model, and it is also offered as Casama Cloud. It has
native apps for iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows, Linux and the web.

**Name.** Casama, with a capital C. Not "Casama AI" in running text, and not "Kasama", its name
before November 2026.

**Logo and screenshots.** The mark and screenshots are in the
[repository](https://github.com/stackwares/casama/tree/main/site/assets). The
[trademark page](https://github.com/stackwares/casama/blob/main/TRADEMARKS.md) says how the name and
logo may be used.
