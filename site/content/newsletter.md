## What you get

Once a month, a short email from the people who make Casama:

- **What shipped.** The new things in the apps and on Casama Cloud, in plain words, with a link to the full [changelog](/changelog) if you want every detail.
- **What's coming next.** What we're working on now, so you can tell us early if it's what you need.
- **One way to use it.** A bot, routine or set-up from the [gallery](https://gallery.casama.ai) or from people using Casama, with the steps to try it yourself.
- **Self-hosting notes.** When a release matters to people who [run Casama on their own server](/self-hosted), such as a security fix or an update to the computer image, the email says so.

It comes from news@news.casama.ai. Replies go to a person at hello@casama.ai, so you can answer any issue with a question or an idea.

## How signing up works

Enter your email above and press **Subscribe**. We send one email with a link. Open it and press **Confirm**, and you're on the list. A short welcome email follows with what to expect. Until you confirm, we don't keep your address, and if you never open the link you won't hear from us again.

Confirmation links work for seven days. If yours has expired, sign up again and we'll send a new one.

## What we never do

- We never add you without asking. Making a Casama account doesn't sign you up.
- We never sell or share the list. It's kept with [Resend](https://resend.com), the service that sends the emails, and used for nothing else.
- We don't track opens or clicks in these emails.
- We send one email a month at most, plus the confirmation and a short welcome when you sign up.

## Stopping

Every email has an **Unsubscribe** link at the bottom. One click and you're off the list, with no reason needed. You can also reply to any issue and ask, or write to [privacy@casama.ai](mailto:privacy@casama.ai) to have your address removed completely. More about how we handle your data is in the [privacy policy](/privacy).

## Other ways to follow along

- The [changelog](/changelog) lists every change, version by version, with its own [feed](/changelog/rss.xml).
- The code and every discussion are on [GitHub](https://github.com/stackwares/casama).
