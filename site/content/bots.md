Every bot in Casama has its own computer, your connected apps, a memory and a schedule. A starter
bot saves you writing its instructions yourself: it comes with the job written down, a few things to
ask it first, and for some a routine, like a briefing every weekday morning. It still asks before
anything risky, such as sending, booking, paying or deleting.

To use one, open **Bots** in the app, tap **New bot** and pick it. Its name fills in, and you can
change the name, the instructions and the picture before you make it. Each one below links to its
page in the gallery, which shows what it asks for and what it can touch.

## Make your own

Start from a blank bot and write what it should do in plain words, or ask a bot you already have to
set one up. You can share a bot as a template, and anyone can add a starter bot to the
[gallery](https://gallery.casama.ai).
