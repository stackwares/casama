## Casama Cloud plans

{{plans}}

Pro starts with a 7-day free trial. Paying yearly costs less: $150 for Plus, $490 for Pro and $899
for Max. For a short project you can also pay by the week: $7.99 for Plus, $19.99 for Pro and
$39.99 for Max, with a week's share of the credits.

## What credits pay for

On Casama Cloud a credit is a dollar. Credits pay for the things that cost money each time a bot
works:

- **AI models.** Every question a bot puts to a model.
- **Cloud computer time** beyond the hours in your plan.
- **Live calls, images, video and read-aloud voices.**
- **Paid data lookups**, which always ask first and show the price.

Every plan includes credits each month. When a busy month needs more, paid plans can buy credit
packs. A bot that is waiting for your approval, or sleeping, costs nothing.

## You stay in charge of spending

Each bot has a budget per run ($1 by default) and per day ($10 by default). When a run reaches its
budget it stops and tells you. The Usage screen shows spend by bot, by model, by day and by where
the work came from, and each reply shows what it cost.

## Self-hosted: $0, forever

Casama is open source under AGPL-3.0. One command installs the whole stack with Docker on a VPS,
a home server or your laptop, and the Mac and Linux app can run a server for you with no terminal.

- No limit on users, bots or computers.
- Use any model: your own keys for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, xAI or OpenRouter, your
  ChatGPT plan, or local models through Ollama. You pay your providers directly, and local models
  cost nothing.
- Casama Link gives your server an address your phone can reach, free on every plan.

See [Run Casama on your own server](/self-hosted).

## Questions

### Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan has $1 of credits every month, 3 bots and one cloud computer with 5 hours of
computer time. It doesn't end.

### What happens when I run out of credits?

Bots stop starting new paid work and tell you why. Nothing is charged without you buying a credit
pack or changing plan.

### Can I add people?

Pro includes 3 seats and Max includes 10. More people cost $12 a month each. Plus and Free are for
one person.

### Do I pay for a run that waits for me?

No. A run that is waiting for your approval costs nothing while it waits.

### Can I move between Cloud and my own server?

Yes. Your workspace exports in full, and bots, skills and routines can be shared as files and
packs.
