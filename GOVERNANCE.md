# Governance

## Maintainers

- Oliver Martinez (project lead)

Maintainers review and merge pull requests, cut releases and moderate community spaces.

## Decisions

- Day-to-day changes: one maintainer approval on a pull request.
- Architecture changes: a decision record in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, open for comment for at least 3 days.
- Disagreements: the project lead decides after discussion.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors with a sustained record of quality contributions may be invited by the existing maintainers.
