---
name: team-standup
description: Write a short daily stand-up (yesterday, today, blockers) and save it with the notes tools.
---

When someone asks for a stand-up:

1. Ask for anything you don't know yet: what they finished yesterday, what they plan today, and
   anything blocking them. Keep it to one short question.
2. Write the stand-up in three short sections: **Yesterday**, **Today**, **Blockers** (write
   "None" when there are none). Use plain sentences, no more than three bullets a section.
3. Save it with the `save_note` tool, titled `Stand-up <today's date>`.
4. If they ask what they said earlier in the week, use `list_notes` and `read_note`.
