# A browser your bots can use on your phone

Your bots already browse on their own computer. On an iPhone, iPad or Android phone you can also
let a bot use a small browser inside Casama, on the phone in your hand, while you watch.

It is for three things:

- **You ask for it.** "Do it on my phone" or "open it here".
- **A site keeps the bot's computer out.** Some sites show a "prove you're human" page to a
  computer in a data center. Your phone usually gets through.
- **A site you signed in to on your phone.** Sign in once, and the bot can work there as you.

For everything else, bots keep using their own computer: it works while you do other things, and
it runs routines.

## Switch it on

1. **Settings → Device Connections → Browser on This Phone.**
2. Read what it does and tap **Allow**.
3. Pick the bots that may use it.

## Sign in to a site for your bots

1. In the same place, tap **Sign In to a Site**.
2. Go to the site and sign in yourself. Nothing you do here is sent to Casama.
3. Tap **Let My Bots Use This Site**.

Sign-ins stay on your phone. Casama's browser can't use your Safari or Chrome sign-ins, so each
site is signed in to once. Some sites (Google's sign-in among them) may refuse a browser inside an
app; for those, the bot asks you to sign in on its own computer instead.

## While a bot works there

- A small card in the chat shows the page. Tap it for the full view.
- **Touch the page and the bot pauses.** Tap **Hand Back** when you're done.
- The first visit to a site asks: **Once**, **Always** or **Never**.
- Before a step that sends, posts, deletes, shares or signs in, the bot asks.
- Purchases and password changes are yours to finish: the bot stops and tells you what's left.
- You type passwords, card numbers and codes yourself. A bot never sees them.
- **Stop** ends it at once.

## Good to know

- It works only while that chat is open on your phone. Leave the chat or the app and the bot
  stops; it may carry on from its own computer.
- One page at a time, no tabs.
- Banking, health, password manager and government ID sites are off. You can switch a kind on;
  it then asks every time.
- **Clear Browsing Data** signs the browser out of everything.
- Needs iOS 17 or later, or a recent Android System WebView. On an older phone the setting says
  it isn't available.
- Workspace admins can switch it off for a workspace: **Workspace settings → Privacy**.
