## A browser your bots can use on your phone

On iPhone, iPad and Android you can switch on a browser inside Casama that your bots can use while
you watch (Settings → Device Connections → Browser on This Phone). It is off until you switch it
on, and you choose which bots may use it.

**What is sent to us.** When a bot works in that browser, what it reads there (the page's text,
its buttons and fields, or a picture of the page) is sent to our server and to the AI model that
bot uses, so the bot can do what you asked. It becomes part of that chat, like any page a bot reads
on its own computer, and is kept and deleted with the chat. We also keep a record of each step
that changed something (opening a page, a click, typing), with the site's name, in your
workspace's audit log.

**What stays on your phone.** Your sign-ins and cookies for that browser stay on your phone, in a
store only that browser uses. They are not sent to our servers. Pages you browse yourself there
(for example to sign in to a site) are not sent to us. A bot never reads what is in a password, card
number or one-time code field.

**Your controls.** A bot can use the browser only while its chat is open on your phone's screen.
You can stop it or take over at any time, remove any site, switch off kinds of site, clear the
browser's data, or switch the whole thing off, in the same place you switched it on. Workspace
admins can switch it off for their workspace.

**What we don't do.** We don't use what a bot reads there for advertising, we don't sell it, and
we don't use it to train AI models.
