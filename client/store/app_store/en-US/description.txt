CHAT WITH BOTS THAT REMEMBER
Each bot has a persona, skills and a memory that carries across conversations. Ask once, and it knows next time.

Every bot has its own sandboxed computer with a shell, files and a browser. Watch it work live and take control whenever you like.

YOU STAY IN CHARGE

Schedule routines or trigger bots from webhooks. Results land in your inbox.

ANY MODEL, YOUR SERVER
