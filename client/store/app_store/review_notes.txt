Casama is an app for AI assistants ("bots"). Bots run on Casama's servers and their own cloud computers; nothing runs on the device.

SIGN IN WITH THE DEMO ACCOUNT
1. Tap "Continue with Email", then "Use a Password Instead".
2. Enter the demo email and password and tap "Sign in". No code or two-step sign-in is needed.
3. The first screen asks to agree that chats go to outside AI providers. Tap "Agree and Continue".
To try registration instead (invite-only), use invite code REVIEW-XXXXXX.
The workspace shows the Free plan but has Pro limits and $50 of credits for review, so every feature works without buying anything.

WHAT TO TRY
- Chat: open the bot "Casama" and ask, e.g. "Find three well-reviewed Italian restaurants in Lisbon and compare them in a table."
- Its computer: while it works, tap the monitor icon in the chat header (idle: chat title > Bot profile > Computer), then "Open Live View". "Take Control" asks for Face ID or your passcode, then pauses the bot; "Hand Back" returns control.
- Approvals: sending, booking, paying or deleting shows a card with Allow / Not Now first.
- Calls: the phone icon in a chat starts a live voice call (needs a microphone).
- Schedules: a "Morning brief" routine is already set up: chat title > Bot profile > Routines. To add one, just ask for it in chat.
- Ready-made bots: Bots > New bot, then pick a persona.
- Group chats: Chats > Group chats > + (needs two bots; the demo has Casama and Anna and a group already). @everyone asks the lead bot to share out the work. Settings > People > Invite by Email adds a person.
- Library (bottom tab) keeps what bots make. Settings has Billing, Privacy and Help & support.
- Needs your own outside account (optional): chat apps (chat title > Bot profile > Chat apps), Gmail/Calendar/Notion (Settings > Integrations).

AI PROVIDERS (5.1.2(i))
Before anything is sent, a full-screen step names who receives what people share with bots: OpenRouter and the model makers behind it (DeepSeek, Google, OpenAI), Google Gemini for calls, Parallel/Serper/DeepSeek for web search. Nothing is sent until "Agree and Continue". It can be withdrawn in Settings > Privacy > AI providers. Image and video requests are moderated first.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
OpenRouter (AI models), Google Gemini Live (calls), RevenueCat (purchases), Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, Composio (Gmail etc.), Firebase (push, crashes), PostHog (analytics; off in Settings > Privacy), ZeptoMail (email).

PURCHASES
Plus, Pro and Max (monthly or yearly) are auto-renewing subscriptions; credit packs are consumables. All are in-app purchases in Settings > Billing. Credit packs show once a workspace subscribes to any plan. Credits from the App Store never expire. Pro has a 1-week free trial for eligible new subscribers.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
- United States storefront only: the paywall also offers web checkout next to in-app purchase, and a workspace admin can buy a website domain with a web payment (Settings > Domains > Find a Domain). Everywhere else: in-app purchase only, and domain buying is hidden.
- Not available in China mainland.

REMOTE SETTINGS
Our server can switch some features off without a release (e.g. domain buying, rating prompt). They only remove features.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > Privacy > Danger Zone > Delete My Account (also casama.ai/delete-account). Please don't delete the demo account.

USER CONTENT
Long-press a reply (right-click on a Mac) > "Report reply". People, bots, gallery items and shared pages can be reported; people can be blocked (Settings > Privacy > Blocked people). Reports go to abuse@casama.ai.

OTHER
- Casama is open source; it can also use a self-run server (the demo uses Casama Cloud).
- Microphone is used for dictation and calls, only when started. Calendar, contacts, health and location go to a bot only if turned on in Settings.
