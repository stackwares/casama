Casama is an app for AI assistants ("bots"). Bots run on Casama's servers and their own sandboxed cloud computers; nothing runs on the device.

SIGN IN WITH THE DEMO ACCOUNT
1. Tap "Continue with Email", then "Use a Password Instead".
2. Enter the demo email and password and tap "Sign in". No code or two-step sign-in is needed.
3. The first screen asks to agree that chats go to outside AI providers. Tap "Agree and Continue".
The workspace shows the Free plan but has Pro limits and $50 of credits for review, so every feature works without buying anything.

WHAT TO TRY
- Chat: open the bot "Casama" and ask, e.g. "Find three well-reviewed Italian restaurants in Lisbon and compare them in a table."
- Its computer: while it works, tap the monitor icon in the chat header, then "Open Live View"; "Take Control" lets you use it yourself.
- Approvals: sending, booking, paying or deleting shows a card with Allow / Not Now first.
- Calls: the phone icon in a chat ("Start a voice call") starts a live voice call.
- Schedules: ask "Every morning at 8, send me a short news brief." Tap the chat title > Bot profile > Routines to see it.
- Ready-made bots: Bots > New bot, then pick a persona.
- Group chats: Chats > New group chat; @everyone asks the lead bot to share out the work. Settings > People > Invite by Email adds a person.
- Library (bottom tab) keeps what bots make. Settings has Billing, Privacy and Help & support.
- Optional, needs the reviewer's own outside account: replies in WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, SMS or email (chat title > Bot profile > Chat apps), Gmail/Calendar/Notion (Settings > Integrations), password managers (Settings > Workspace settings > Advanced).

AI PROVIDERS (5.1.2(i))
Before anything is sent, a full-screen step names who receives what people share with bots: OpenRouter and the model makers behind it (DeepSeek, Google, OpenAI), Google Gemini for calls, and Parallel/Serper/DeepSeek for web search. Nothing is sent until "Agree and Continue". It can be withdrawn in Settings > Privacy > AI providers. Image and video requests are checked by a moderation step first.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
OpenRouter (AI models), Google Gemini Live (calls), RevenueCat (in-app purchases), Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In and passkeys, Composio (connections to Gmail etc.), Firebase (push, crash reports), PostHog (analytics; can be turned off in Settings > Privacy), ZeptoMail (account email).

PURCHASES
Plus, Pro and Max (monthly or yearly) are auto-renewing subscriptions; credit packs are consumables. All are in-app purchases in Settings > Billing. Credit packs show once a workspace subscribes to any plan (a sandbox purchase shows them). Credits from the App Store never expire. Pro has a 1-week free trial for eligible new subscribers.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
- United States storefront only: the paywall also offers web checkout next to in-app purchase, and a workspace admin can buy a website domain with a web payment (Settings > Domains > Find a Domain). Everywhere else: in-app purchase only, and domain buying is hidden.
- Not available in China mainland.

REMOTE SETTINGS
Our server can switch features off without a release (domain buying in App Store builds, the rating prompt, maintenance mode, a minimum version). They only remove features; none turns on anything that isn't in this build.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > Privacy > Danger Zone > Delete My Account (also https://casama.ai/delete-account). Please don't delete the demo account.

USER CONTENT
Long-press a reply (right-click on a Mac) > "Report reply". People, bots, gallery items and shared pages can be reported; people can be blocked (Settings > Privacy > Blocked people). Reports go to abuse@casama.ai.

OTHER
- Casama is open source; the app can also connect to a self-run server. The demo uses Casama Cloud.
- Microphone is used for dictation and calls, only when started. Calendar, contacts, health and location go to a bot only if turned on in Settings.
