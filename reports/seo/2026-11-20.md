## Not fixable by editing the site

- **No GitHub stars, no referring domains.** The AI engines cite GitHub 44 times in 115 answers and our
  repo has 0 stars. This is the main reason Casama is named in 0 answers. It moves with the launch
  (Show HN, Product Hunt, r/selfhosted, drafts in `docs/launch/`) and with the roundup notes
  (`docs/launch/roundup-notes.md`), which come from Oliver's mailbox.
- **Wikidata.** Re-create the item only after independent sources cover Casama (stars, press, a
  third-party review); a second deletion is likely before that.
- **GA4 can't tell where visits came from** (99% "not set"). The site counter is the source of truth
  for visits by source, by design (no cookies).
- **Rank tracker** has not run yet; its first check is 2026-11-24, the AI answers on 2026-11-25.
