# Product Hunt

**Name:** Kasama
**Tagline (60):** AI teammates with memory and their own computers
**Description (260):** Kasama gives you bots that remember, work on real sandboxed computers, and ask
before risky steps. Chat from your phone or desktop, watch them work live, take control anytime.
Open source: self-host with one script and any model, or use Kasama Cloud.

**First comment:** Why we built it, what self-hosting looks like (install.sh, about 15 minutes),
what "ask before risky" means in practice, and what's next (channels: Telegram, email, Slack).

**Gallery:** chat with a tool call; live computer view with Take control; approval notification;
routines; self-host terminal; the 60-second demo.
