**Name:** Casama

**Description (260):** Casama gives you bots that remember, work on real sandboxed computers, and ask
before risky steps. Chat from your phone or desktop, watch them work live, take control anytime.

what "ask before risky" means in practice (payments always ask; a checkpoint is saved before
destructive commands), that bots also answer in Telegram, Slack, WhatsApp and email, and that a
server at home is reachable from your phone through Casama Link with no domain. Link the honest
comparison: https://casama.ai/compare/best-open-source-ai-agents. Say what's weak: it's new, and
on Cloud Casama picks the model.

**Pricing line:** Free to self-host. Cloud: Free, then $15, $49 or $99 a month.
