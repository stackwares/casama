Apple Watch · the app

One list, most urgent first, and one big button to ask. Amber is only ever "needs you", green is only ever "working". Every screen is readable in two seconds and finished in one tap.

Apple Watch · notifications

Most of what people do on a watch starts with a tap on the wrist. Each kind gets its own layout and buttons that finish the job on the watch.

Apple Watch · the watch face, the Smart Stack and controls

Knowing without opening anything. Complications show the count and the next thing; the Smart Stack brings the right card up by itself when a bot needs you or is working.

Wear OS · the app

The same screens, in the watch's own language: round layouts, a button that hugs the bottom edge, colours that follow the watch face, and the crown or bezel to scroll.

Wear OS · tiles, the watch face and notifications

Tiles are one swipe from the watch face. Each answers one question and has one button.

States both watches share

A watch is often offline, half asleep or in public. Each of those has a screen that is still true and still useful.
