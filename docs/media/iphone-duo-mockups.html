Closed: the outer screen

iOS keeps an 84-point column down the right edge for the camera, the clock and the battery. Casama puts its buttons and tabs under them, where Apple's own apps have theirs, so the page gets the full height.

Open, wide: two panes that meet on the fold

The list is as wide as the outer screen, so it looks the same on both. The chat is as wide as an ordinary iPhone. The strip and its buttons have not moved.

Book: partly folded

Nothing rearranges. A 40-point band opens around the fold and stays empty, and anything that pops up does so on the right half.

The bot's computer beside the chat

The monitor button swaps the list for the bot's computer, the same panel desktop shows beside a chat.

Laptop: propped up, the fold across the middle

The top half is for what you watch, the bottom half for what you touch.

Tent, and open tall

Standing on its edges, the outer screen is for hands-free use. Held tall and flat, the inner screen goes back to the phone layout, as Apple's apps do.
